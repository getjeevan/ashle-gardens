# 🌿 Ashle Gardens — Integrated Heritage Campus, Madurai

**Site:** J-farm, Meenakshipuram, Kariapatti Taluk, Virudhunagar District, TN
**Coordinates:** 9.7212°N, 78.0852°E | 22.4 km S of Madurai | 12.7 km from IXM Airport
**Scale:** 30–40 acres | NH-85 corridor (Madurai–Tuticorin highway)

## Vision
A country-club-style retirement community that funds itself through an integrated circular
economy campus — where every cost centre is also a profit centre, and where rural youth
become skilled entrepreneurs through a live-work-research Centre of Excellence.

## 10 Business Units
1. **Retirement Community** — heritage rooms, capsule Airbnb, lake, island, water streams
2. **Commercial Laundry** — B2B to Madurai hospitals, hotels, Sivakasi industry
3. **Commercial Kitchen** — catering, hospital canteens, home delivery, cloud kitchen
4. **Eco-tourism** — day visits, MICE, school programs, heritage walks, farm experiences
5. **Biochar Production** — farm biomass → soil amendment + carbon credits + exports
6. **Biogas Plant** — organic waste → kitchen fuel + electricity offset
7. **Organic Farm** — zero-mile produce for kitchen + members + CSA subscriptions
8. **COE (Centre of Excellence)** — Pro bono innovation residency for brilliant young minds. They solve real problems for Ashle Gardens portfolio. No stipend — they get free stay, meals, mentorship, ₹50K prototype budget, and the right to commercialise their solution.
9. **Ayurveda Spa & Wellness** — retreat programs, day spa, naturopathy
10. **NRI Concierge Services**
11. **Co-working & Research Residency** — stay-and-work campus for entrepreneurs, research scholars, remote workers. Short-term and monthly options.
12. **Pet Spa & Hostel** — premium pet grooming (incl. Ayurveda pet treatments), boarding, and pet-friendly eco-stay. ₹1.14 Cr/yr, 74% EBITDA margin. — stay-and-work for entrepreneurs, research scholars, remote workers. Short-term and monthly options. — return-to-India advisory, property management, admin

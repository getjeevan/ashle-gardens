# Biochar Production Plan — Ashle Gardens
*Velu, Circular Economy Lead | Kariapatti, Virudhunagar*

## Technology Selection: Adam Retort Kiln

Recommended over alternatives for Ashle Gardens scale:

| Technology | CAPEX | Output | Quality | Training |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TLUD gasifier | ₹80K–1.5L | 200–400 kg/day | Variable | Easy |
| **Adam retort kiln** | ₹4–6L | 300–600 kg/day | Consistent | Moderate |
| Continuous pyrolysis | ₹15–25L | 1–3T/day | Commercial | Complex |

**Why Adam retort:** Proven design, locally fabricable in Virudhunagar (metal workshops in Virudhunagar/Rajapalayam), COE students can operate and research improvements.

## Feedstock Sources

| Feedstock | Source (distance) | Availability | Seasonal? | Yield |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coconut shell | Local farms, Kariapatti/Tirumangalam (5–15 km) | Year-round | No | 28–32% |
| Sugarcane bagasse | Virudhunagar/Rajapalayam sugar mills (20–30 km) | Oct–Feb | Yes | 20–25% |
| Palm frond waste | Campus trees + nearby farms | Year-round | No | 20–22% |
| Neem/moringa branches | Campus pruning (annual) | Feb–Mar | Yes | 25% |
| Groundnut shells | Virudhunagar district farms (large producer) | Nov–Jan | Yes | 30% |

**Year-round supply strategy:** Coconut shell (base load) + palm fronds + campus pruning = ~200 kg/day base feedstock. Seasonal supplementation from bagasse/groundnut shells to reach 400+ kg/day.

## IBI Certification Path
1. Produce 5 tonnes minimum for testing
2. Send samples to accredited lab: SGS India (Chennai), Intertek, or TERI (Delhi)
3. Tests: pH, electrical conductivity, moisture, fixed carbon (>10%), PAH (polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons <12 mg/kg), heavy metals
4. Application fee: USD 500 to International Biochar Initiative (IBI, Switzerland)
5. Timeline: 3–4 months after lab results
6. Annual recertification fee: USD 300

## Verra VCS Carbon Credit Pathway
1. Register as Project Developer on Verra registry (verra.org)
2. Choose methodology: VM0044 (Biochar carbon removal)
3. Prepare Project Design Document (PDD) — 40–60 page technical document; can hire a consultant (₹3–5L one-time)
4. Validation by a Verra-approved third party (cost: USD 10,000–20,000)
5. Monitoring: annual reporting of biochar production volumes
6. Credit issuance: ~0.8–1.2 tonnes CO₂e per tonne of biochar produced
7. Credit price: USD 15–50 (expect USD 20–30 for IBI-certified biochar credits)
8. **At 280 kg/day (Year 2):** ~100 tonnes biochar/year × 1.0 t CO₂e = 100 credits × USD 25 = **USD 2,500/year (~₹2.1L/year)**
   - Not huge revenue but grows with scale and carbon market appreciation

## Export Route
- Pack in 25 kg HDPE bags (IBI logo + certificate number)
- Aggregate at Virudhunagar inland container depot (ICD Virudhunagar, 25 km)
- Ship via Thoothukudi (V.O. Chidambaranar) Port, 95 km — container to Rotterdam/Hamburg
- Target markets: EU organic agriculture (Germany, Netherlands, Austria)
- Export documentation: IBI certificate, phytosanitary (not required for biochar), FSSAI/APEDA if marketed as soil amendment

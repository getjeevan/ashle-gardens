# Madurai Climate Summary

This document summarizes the climate conditions in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India, relevant to the design of the retirement community.

## General Information

*   **Latitude/longitude:** ~9.92°N 78.12°E, elevation ~140m
*   **Climate:** BSh (semi-arid hot)
*   **Annual mean temperature:** 28°C
    *   April–June peaks: 38–42°C
    *   Dec–Jan lows: 18–22°C
*   **Rainfall:** ~850 mm/yr
    *   NE monsoon (Oct–Dec) dominant (~55%)
    *   SW monsoon (Jun–Sep) ~30%
*   **Soils:** Red sandy loam in higher terrain, black cotton (vertisol) in lower plains, alluvial near Vaigai
*   **Vegetation zone:** Tropical thorn forest transitioning to dry deciduous

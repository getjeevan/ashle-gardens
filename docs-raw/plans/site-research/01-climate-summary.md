## 12. Implications for Ashle Gardens Design

### Water Cycle
1. **Dry season (Feb–May):** Rainfall ~50 mm/month; ET ~200+ mm/month → **net deficit 150 mm/month**. Irrigation must come from stored water (lake, tank, borewell).
2. **Monsoon (Jun–Sep & Oct–Dec):** Rainfall ~300 mm/month on average; quick-capture tanks required; spillway design critical.
3. **Annual balance:** ~750 mm rainfall on 40 acres (~0.5 acre-m) + borewell allowance + recycled greywater → must meet ~1970 mm ET demand + 80 occupants + landscape beauty.

### Thermal Design
1. **Cold months (Nov–Feb):** Comfort outdoors; maximize solar gain on S–E facing; minimal cooling.
2. **Hot months (Mar–Jun):** Peak outdoor temps 35–42°C; capsules must have 6–8°C passive cooling buffer (earth tubes, double skin, deep eaves).
3. **Monsoon (Jun–Sep):** Humid; cross-ventilation critical; no air stratification.

### Flora & Shade
1. **May–Jun:** Peak deciduous leaf-fall; design for temporary shade loss; prioritize evergeen mid-story (mango, neem mixed).
2. **Jul–Sep:** Green canopy returns; heavy rainfall; ensure good drainage under tree clusters.
3. **Nov–Feb:** Mild; native flowering species bloom (tamarind, pongam, Indian mast tree); attract birds & pollinators.

### Crop & Food Production
1. **Year-round:** Curry leaf, drumstick, banana (high ET, but feasible with drip); tulasi, mint (shade-tolerant).
2. **Monsoon (Jun–Sep):** Vegetable gardens (greens, beans, okra); pulse crops if mulched.
3. **Post-monsoon (Oct–Jan):** Primary sowing window for rabi crops (chickpea, lentil, fenugreek); tree fruiting (mango, tamarind, guava).
4. **Summer irrigation (Feb–May):** Drip-fed; fruit trees in stress, minimize new sowings unless high-value.

## Data Sources & Caveats

- **IMD (India Meteorological Department):** 30-year normals (1991–2020), Madurai Station (9.92°N, 78.12°E).
- **ET₀ (FAO-56 Penman-Monteith):** Calculated from sunshine, temperature, RH, wind speed; adjustments for monsoon cloud cover applied per regional studies.
- **Soil data:** ICAR–TNAU surveys; Virudhunagar District Agricultural Directorate (published soil series maps).
- **Groundwater:** Central Groundwater Board (CGWB) Madurai–Sivagangai surveys; private borewell data from farmers in Kariapatti taluk.
- **Hazards:** IMD cyclone tracks, flood records, drought declaration history (Tamil Nadu Revenue Dept).
- **Variability:** This is a semi-arid zone with high inter-annual variance (~20% on rainfall); single-site design must plan for 1-in-5 drought and 1-in-10 flood.

## ⚠️ Critical Finding — Nanjai (Wet) Land

**96.4% of documented land is classified as NANJAI (wet/irrigated agricultural land).**
Irrigation source: C = Canal (Kariapatti tank/ery system)

This is more complex to convert than Punjai (dry) land:
- Wet land conversion requires additional PWD/Irrigation Department NOC
- Canal-command-area land needs WRO (Water Resources Organisation) clearance
- Nanjai ceiling is lower under TN Land Reforms Act
- Factor ~3–4 extra months in conversion timeline vs dry land

### 1. This is ~4.6 acres, not 30–40 acres
The documented pattas cover only **4.595 acres**. The Ashle Gardens vision requires 30–40 acres. Either:
- Additional parcels from other families need to be acquired (likely adjacent survey numbers)
- Or this is the seed/anchor parcel and more land is being negotiated

### 2. Three separate patta holders = three separate sale deeds
- Patta 56: One sale deed with Alagiri Nayakkar (or legal heirs)
- Patta 113: One sale deed — **BOTH** Silukka Nayakkar heirs AND Vellaisamy Nayakkar must co-sign
- Patta 446: One sale deed with Perumal

### 3. Nanjai (wet land) conversion — additional steps required
Standard land conversion (Section 27/28) PLUS:
- **WRO (Water Resources Organisation) / PWD NOC** — canal-command area clearance
- Confirm with Kariapatti Tahsildar if this falls under any irrigation scheme
- Check if any D-number references (D2002/6 in some A-registers) indicate legal disputes or prior encumbrances
- The "D2002" notation on some parcels in Patta 113 needs investigation — may indicate a court case or division order from 2002

### 4. Verify before purchase
Essential checks before finalising:
- [ ] EC (Encumbrance Certificate) for last 30 years — all 3 pattas

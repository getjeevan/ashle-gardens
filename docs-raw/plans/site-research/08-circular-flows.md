# Campus Circular Economy Flows — Ashle Gardens
*Velu, Circular Economy Lead | Complete material flow map*

## Philosophy
Every output from one system is the input for another. Nothing leaves the campus as waste.

---

## Waste That Leaves the Campus

| Waste stream | Volume | Destination | Revenue/cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surplus biochar | 200–400 kg/day | Sold to farms, nurseries, export | ₹3–6L/month revenue |
| Treated laundry effluent (30% not recycled) | 14,000 L/day | Deep root irrigation (only) — NOT to lake | Zero cost |
| Non-compostable waste (plastics, metal) | 5–8 kg/day | Municipal collection, Kariapatti | ₹500/month cost |
| Medical/hazardous (from pet spa, wellness) | <1 kg/day | Authorised medical waste contractor | ₹2,000/month cost |

**Campus is 95% zero-waste.** Only 5–8 kg/day of non-recyclable material leaves for municipal disposal.

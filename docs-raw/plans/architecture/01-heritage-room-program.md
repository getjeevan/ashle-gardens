## Madurai Heritage Architecture Reference Points
- **Mutram (central courtyard):** Open-to-sky, holds tulasi maadam, morning yoga space
- **Thinnai (raised verandah):** Semi-public transition zone, traditional hospitality space  
- **Padikattu (stepped plinth):** Entry ritual, flood resilience, visual hierarchy
- **Teak columns:** Profile from Madurai Meenakshi temple pillar motifs — octagonal base
- **Athangudi tile:** Made in Athangudi village (70km from Madurai), naturally cool, unique patterns
- **Lime plaster:** Breathes in summer heat, traditional finish, low maintenance

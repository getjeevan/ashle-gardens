## THE EXPERIENCE: 2-DAY SAMPLE ITINERARY

### Audience: IT Company Leadership Team (30 people)
**Theme:** "Lead Differently" — Heritage wisdom meets sustainability innovation

#### **Day 1: Arrival & Immersion (2 PM–9 PM)**

**2:00–3:00 PM: Arrival & Welcome**
- Check-in at heritage rooms (AC/fan, traditional architecture)
- Welcome drink (jigarthanda or fresh juice)
- Brief orientation (wifi, facilities, emergency info)

**3:00–4:15 PM: Heritage Walk & Architecture Talk**
- Guided tour of Chettinad courtyard, explaining 100-year-old design
- Why courtyards matter (cooling, light, social space)
- Analogy: **"How colonial-era wisdom informs modern workplace design"**
- Stop for tea in heritage pavilion

**4:30–5:45 PM: Biochar Lab Experience (Hands-On)**
- Visit working biochar kiln
- Demo: biomass → carbon sequestration process
- Small group activity: Sort + prepare materials for biochar batch
- Take-home sample + brief: "Permanent carbon storage—what businesses can learn"
- **Engagement angle:** Turn CSR obligation into R&D curiosity

**6:00–7:00 PM: Farm Walk & Permaculture Storytelling**
- Walk active farm; see today's harvest prepped for dinner
- Story: How permaculture mimics nature's efficiency
- **Parallel:** Organizational design principles (diversity, redundancy, resilience)
- Photo op: sunset from lake vantage point

**7:30–9:00 PM: Chettinad Seven-Course Dinner**
- Candlelit outdoor meal (if weather permits; covered pavilion backup)
- Menu: Madurai-Tamil classics cooked by Chef Selvi + COE culinary students
- Informal storytelling: Local food traditions, ingredient sourcing
- Network opportunity (no forced icebreakers; natural mingling)

**9:00 PM–:** Free time
- Lounge, rest, or stargazing from capsule house decks

---

#### **Day 2: Strategy & Sustainability (8 AM–12:30 PM)**

**8:00–9:00 AM: Sunrise Yoga & Reflection**
- Optional yoga in heritage pavilion (or nature walk for non-yoga folks)
- Setting: Lake views, birdsong, quiet start to deep work

**9:00–10:00 AM: Breakfast & Regrouping**
- Farm-fresh breakfast (eggs from campus chickens, vegetables from garden, bread from farmhouse oven)
- Informal conversation encouraged

**10:00 AM–12:00 PM: Facilitated Leadership Workshop**
- **"Why Heritage Teaches Us About Organizational Resilience"**
  - Framing: Chettinad merchants thrived for 200 years through relationships + adaptation
  - Discussion: How your organization builds resilience in uncertainty
  - Optional breakout: Small team strategy sessions in different heritage rooms (natural breakout spaces)
  - Facilitator: External leadership coach (optional) or Deepa (culture-focused conversation)
- **Activity:** Each team defines one "heritage principle" they take back (e.g., "Build Resilience Through Diversity")

**12:00–12:30 PM: Closing Circle & Commitments**
- Group gathers in courtyard
- Each team shares one commitment from the offsite
- Group photo at heritage gate
- Certificates of participation (optional branding)

**12:30 PM: Lunch & Departure**
- Final farm-to-table meal
- Checkout; drivers arranged
- Thank-you gift: Small biochar sample + Madurai heritage postcard

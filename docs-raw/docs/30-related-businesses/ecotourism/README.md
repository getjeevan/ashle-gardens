### 3. [03-mice-pitch.md](./03-mice-pitch.md)
**Purpose:** Sales & marketing strategy for corporate MICE programs

**What It Contains:**
- **The 30-Second Pitch:** Core value proposition
- **Problem & Solution:** Why corporate offsites are broken; why Ashle Gardens fixes it
- **Why Ashle Gardens (6 key differentiators)**
  - Heritage authenticity (100-year Chettinad courtyard, not theme-park)
  - Sustainability credibility (working labs, not greenwashing)
  - Strategic B2B model (high-value, white-glove, not volume chase)
  - Pricing advantage (40–50% cheaper than 5-star resorts)
- **Sample 2-Day Itinerary** (detail, hour-by-hour)
  - Arrival → Heritage walk → Biochar lab → Farm → Dinner → Yoga → Strategy workshop → CSR volunteer
  - Optional CSR component (company works with COE students)
- **Three Pricing Tiers:**
  - Team Building Day: ₹4,000/person
  - Leadership Retreat: ₹5,500/person
  - CSR/Team Camp (3-day): ₹8,000/person
  - Custom bulk discounts (40+ people, 3+ days)
- **Target Markets (4 Segments):**
  1. **IT & Tech** (Chennai, Bengaluru) — CSR mandate, meaning-seeking
  2. **Management Consulting & Startups** — Culture-building, team bonding
  3. **Sivakasi & Local Industries** — Environmental CSR, worker retention
  4. **Financial Services & Family Offices** — Wellness, team cohesion
- **Competitive Positioning:** Ashle vs. 5-star resorts, vs. adventure resorts
- **Sales Collateral Templates:**
  - 1-page brochure (email-friendly)
  - Outreach email templates + follow-ups
- **Booking & Operations:** 6–10 week lead time, deposit/cancellation terms
- **Expected Outcomes:** Team engagement ROI, CSR impact metrics, revenue projections
- **Launch Strategy (4 Phases):** Soft launch Jun–Jul → Market testing Aug–Sep → Full launch Oct–Dec → Scale 2027

**Use This For:**
- Sales team: Email templates, pitch angles per segment, pricing talking points
- Marketing: Branding, social media angle (authenticity + purpose), event strategy
- Business development: Identifying prospects, partnership opportunities
- Finance: Revenue targets (₹1.5–3L per booking, 1–2/week = ₹1.5–2.5Cr annual)

# Co-working & Research Residency — Ashle Gardens

> **Business Unit 11** (added 2026-05-16 by founder)

## Concept

A **stay-and-work campus** within Ashle Gardens for entrepreneurs, research scholars, freelancers, and remote-work professionals who want to combine a productive environment with the Madurai heritage experience.

This is not just a co-working space — it is a **work residency on a living circular economy campus**, where your workspace is surrounded by a 3-acre lake, a permaculture farm, a biochar production unit, and a commercial kitchen producing heritage Madurai cuisine for lunch.

**Tagline:** *Work from somewhere worth coming back to.*

## Target residents

| Profile | Why Ashle Gardens |
|---|---|
| **Early-stage entrepreneur** | Cheap rural base, COE network, quiet focus, Madurai market access |
| **PhD / research scholar** | Live-in lab access (farm, biogas, biochar, water systems), publish real data |
| **Remote-working NRI** | Return to Tamil Nadu roots, explore retirement community concept first-hand |
| **Social entrepreneur** | Collaborate with COE residents, test solutions in a live circular economy |
| **Freelancer / digital nomad** | Serene environment, fast internet, heritage aesthetic, affordable |
| **Startup team (2-5 people)** | Private work pod, shared lab, on-campus accommodation, focus retreat |

## Product tiers

| Tier | Duration | What's included | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Day Pass** | 1 day | Hot desk, WiFi, lunch, campus access | ₹800/day |

*Prices include meals from Ashle kitchen (zero-mile food). Internet: dedicated 100 Mbps leased line.*

## Workspace design

### COE Innovation Lab (shared with COE residents)
- 1,500 sqft open floor
- 30 hot desks + 5 focus pods + 2 meeting rooms (8-person each)
- Maker corner: 3D printer, laser cutter, basic electronics bench
- Whiteboard walls, projector, video conferencing
- Rooftop deck overlooking the lake — for calls, thinking, informal meetings

### Private Cabins (for teams)
- 4 private work cabins, each 200 sqft, 4 desks
- Air-conditioned, soundproofed, private entrance
- Scenic view: one overlooks lake, one overlooks farm, two overlook garden

### Outdoor work nodes
- Shaded pavilion near the lake (ideal for laptop work in cool season)
- Thinnai (traditional Tamil verandah) work nooks across heritage buildings
- Island gazebo (for calls, writing, focus time)

## Why this works for Ashle Gardens (financials)

### Revenue
- Day passes: 10/day × ₹800 × 300 days = **₹24L/year**
- Weekly sprints: 5/week × ₹4,500 × 40 weeks = **₹9L/year**
- Monthly residents: 15 desks × ₹22,000 × 12 months = **₹39.6L/year**
- Research residencies: 4 × ₹35,000 × 12 months = **₹16.8L/year**
- Team cabins: 2 cabins × ₹55,000 × 10 months = **₹11L/year**
- **Total potential: ₹1-1.2 Cr/year**

### What it costs Ashle Gardens (already paid)
- WiFi is shared infrastructure (campus anyway)
- Meals come from kitchen (marginal cost ₹120/person vs ₹800+ pricing)
- Capsule houses are dual-use (co-working residents at night, eco-tourism by day for short stays)

### Strategic value beyond revenue
1. **Fills capsule houses in off-season** when eco-tourism is slower
2. **Adds innovation energy** to the campus — keeps it feeling alive and purposeful
3. **NRI discovery pathway** — many NRI remote workers experience Ashle Gardens for a month and return as retirement buyers or investors
4. **PR and press** — "India's first circular economy co-working residency" is a story that writes itself
5. **COE cross-pollination** — co-working residents and COE residents collaborate; some become co-founders

## Connection to COE
The co-working space and COE Innovation Lab are **the same physical space**, shared in shifts:
- Mornings (7am–12pm): COE residents have priority lab access (hands-on building)
- Afternoons (12pm–7pm): Co-working residents use the space (focused desk work)
- Evenings (6pm–9pm): Shared — community dinners, talks, informal collaboration

This intentional overlap creates the **serendipitous collision** that turns a co-working space into an innovation ecosystem.

## 1. Comparison Table: Section 8 vs Trust vs Registered Society

| Criteria | Section 8 Company | Trust | Registered Society |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Legal Framework** | Companies Act, 2013 | Indian Trusts Act, 1882 | Societies Registration Act, 1860 |
| **Governance** | Board of Directors (3–10) | Trustees (2–5) | Managing Committee (7–15) |
| **Compliance burden** | High (Annual MCA filing, audit, ROC oversight) | Low (simple trust deed, minimal filing) | Medium (Annual registration, audit if >₹10L) |
| **Profit distribution** | PROHIBITED (core feature) | Distribution per deed (typically not for profit) | Surplus reinvested only |
| **Taxation** | 80G exemption eligible; full CSR compliance | 80G eligible; minimal IT compliance | 80G eligible; IT exemption u/s 12A |
| **Foreign funding** | FCRA registration (Ministry of Home Affairs) | FCRA registration possible | FCRA registration possible |
| **Donor confidence** | HIGH — regulated, transparent, auditable | MEDIUM — less formal | MEDIUM — formal but dated framework |
| **Bank loans** | Possible (with collateral) | Difficult | Difficult |
| **Staff employment** | Formal (PF, ESI, statutory benefits) | Informal possible | Formal typical |
| **Perpetual succession** | YES (survives founder/donor deaths) | NO (ends with last trustee unless new deed) | YES (survives member turnover) |
| **Incorporation cost** | ₹10,000–25,000 (MCA21 + RoC + legal) | ₹2,000–5,000 (deed registration, stamp) | ₹3,000–8,000 (registration + bylaws) |
| **Timeline (full setup)** | 8–12 weeks | 2–3 weeks | 4–6 weeks |
| **Suitable for** | Formal partnerships, large funding rounds, CSR | Small, founder-led initiatives | Community-driven, membership model |

## 2. Recommended Path for Ashle Gardens COE

### **RECOMMENDATION: Section 8 Company**

**Rationale:**

1. **PMKVY & NSDC alignment** — NSDC requires registered legal entity; Section 8 Company is the gold standard for skill development centers in India.

2. **Dual-funding capacity** — Can simultaneously access:
   - PMKVY 4.0 grants (₹15,000–35,000 per student trained)
   - CSR funds from Sivakasi fireworks companies, textile mills
   - State TN Skill Development schemes
   - International funding (GIZ, Aga Khan Foundation, UNDP)

3. **80G + FCRA combination** — Unlocks:
   - Tax deduction for domestic donors (80G u/s 80G)
   - Foreign funding for international partnerships (FCRA)
   - Corporate match-funding from CSR-compliant companies

4. **Apprenticeship legal clarity** — Section 8 Company structure ensures:
   - Clear employment classification for apprentices (Apprentices Act 1961 compliance)
   - Separate profit center for laundry/farm operations
   - Ring-fenced skill development spend (protected from commercial pressure)

5. **Scale & perpetuity** — If Prof. Sundaram retires or founders pass:
   - Entity survives (perpetual succession)
   - Continuity for partnerships with ITIs, polytechnics, employers
   - Board governs, not individuals

**Why not Trust or Society?**
- **Trust:** Too informal; NSDC may not accept; foreign funding harder; no perpetuity if sole trustee passes.
- **Society:** Antiquated governance; limited CSR access; less credible for PMKVY partnerships.

## 3. Incorporation Steps for Section 8 Company

### **Phase 1: Pre-Filing (Weeks 1–2)**

#### 3.1 Define Articles of Association
- **Clause 1:** Non-distribution clause (core Section 8 requirement)
  > "No dividend, gift, donation, or distribution of surplus shall be made to members or founders. All surplus shall be reinvested in the COE mission."
- **Clause 2:** Dissolution clause
  > "Upon dissolution, all assets transfer to another registered charity or government body (e.g., TNSDC)."
- **Clause 3:** Skill development mission statement
  > "To operate a Centre of Excellence for vocational skill development, apprenticeship training, and R&D in partnership with ITI/Polytechnic institutions."
- **Clause 4:** Governance structure
  > "Board of 5–7 directors (including external experts, ITI principals, employer representatives)."

#### 3.2 Decide on Board Structure
Propose:
- **Chairperson:** Prof. Sundaram (or nominated COE head)
- **Director 1:** HR/Admin head from Ashle Gardens
- **Director 2:** Senior ITI principal (e.g., Govt. ITI Madurai)
- **Director 3:** Industry representative (e.g., Sivakasi CSR lead or textile mill HR)
- **Director 4:** Finance/Audit (external CA or retired bank official)
- **Director 5:** External expert (AICTE liaison, NSDC representative, or skill development NGO lead)

#### 3.3 Obtain PAN & TAN
- File Form 49AA with nearest Income Tax office
- Generate Company PAN (online within 5–7 days via ITR portal)
- Post-incorporation, apply for TAN (Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number)

#### 3.4 Secure Registered Office Address
- Fixed office address within Tamil Nadu (Kariapatti or Madurai office acceptable)
- Landlord's NOC if rented; title deed copy if owned
- Address must be consistent for 1 year before MCA filing

---

### **Phase 2: MCA21 E-Filing (Weeks 3–5)**

#### 3.5 File Incorporation Application on MCA21 Portal
**Steps:**
1. Visit **https://mca.gov.in** → MCA21 Portal → File Now → E-Filing
2. Select: **"Incorporation under Companies Act 2013 (Private Ltd / OPC / Section 8 Company)"**
3. Choose: **"Section 8 Company (Not-for-Profit)"**
4. Fill Form **INC-32** (online form):
   - Company name (e.g., "Ashle Gardens Centre of Excellence Private Limited")
   - Directors' details (PAN, DIN — Designated Identification Number)
   - Registered office address (Kariapatti)
   - Articles & Memorandum of Association (upload pre-drafted PDFs)
   - Board resolution for MOA/AOA
5. Upload required documents:
   - Affidavit by proposer director (notarized)
   - Proof of registered office (lease deed / ownership certificate)
   - Director identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN)
   - Consent letters from all directors

#### 3.6 Obtain Director Identification Number (DIN) for Each Director
- **Online (fastest):** Visit MCA21 → Apply for DIN
- Form IND-1 (e-form)
- Takes 1–2 days (online)
- Cost: **Free**
- Directors must have separate DIN; Prof. Sundaram cannot use same DIN for multiple companies

#### 3.7 Receive RoC Approval
- RoC (Registrar of Companies) reviews within **7–14 days**
- If approved: receive **Certificate of Incorporation**
- If pending queries: respond via MCA21 portal within 10 days
- Approval triggers email with:
  - Corporate Identification Number (CIN)
  - Incorporation certificate (PDF)

---

### **Phase 3: Post-Incorporation (Weeks 6–8)**

#### 3.8 Statutory Filings
1. **Board Meeting (Within 30 days of incorporation):**
   - Appoint Company Secretary (optional but recommended)
   - Appoint Auditor (Chartered Accountant)
   - Authorize bank account opening
   - Approve first financial year budget
   - File **Form INC-3** (Board Resolutions summary)

2. **Bank Account Opening:**
   - Visit bank with:
     - Incorporation certificate (original + 2 copies)
     - Board resolution for bank account
     - Director ID proofs
     - NOC from RoC (if required by bank)
   - Open current account in company name
   - Cost: **Nil** (bank may charge nominal account opening fee ₹0–2,000)

3. **PF & ESI Registration (if employing staff):**
   - File with EPFO (Employee Provident Fund Organization)
   - File with state Labor Department (ESI)
   - Required to formalize apprentice employment

#### 3.9 80G Registration (Tax Exemption)
- File **Form 10G** with nearest Income Tax office
- Requires:
  - Incorporation certificate
  - Bylaws & MOA (notarized copies)
  - Self-certification of non-profit status
  - Auditor's certificate
  - Latest audited financials (if >1 year old)
- Processing: **30–60 days**
- Once approved: donors can claim **50–100% tax deduction** on donations
- Cost: **Nil**

#### 3.10 FCRA Registration (For International Funding)
- Apply to **Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)** → FCRA Wing
- Form: **FCRA-1** (online via https://fcra.gov.in)
- Requires:
  - 80G certificate (must be obtained first)
  - MOA, bylaws, bank details
  - Proof of past CSR/skill development work (Ashle Gardens track record)
  - Anticipated foreign contributions (budget forecast)
- Processing: **60–90 days**
- Once approved: can receive foreign funding for COE activities
- Cost: **Nil** (government registration; nominal FCRA compliance filing ~₹1,000 annually)

## 6. 80G Application Path (Detailed)

### 6.1 Eligibility Check
Section 8 Company automatically qualifies IF:
- ✅ Non-distribution clause in bylaws
- ✅ Registered office in India
- ✅ Annual financial statements audited
- ✅ Skill development/education is primary mission (per MOA)

### 6.2 Documentation Required

1. **Self-certification letter**
   - On company letterhead
   - Statement: "Company operates exclusively for charitable purpose (skill development); no profits distributed to members."

2. **Bylaws & MOA** (notarized)
   - Specifically highlight non-distribution clause
   - Highlight dissolution clause (assets to another charity)

3. **Auditor's Certificate**
   - Format: "I certify that the accounts have been prepared in accordance with Accounting Standards and no profit has been distributed to members in FY [year]."

4. **Bank statement** (previous 12 months)
   - Proof of funding sources (CSR deposits, donations, grants)
   - Proof of expense (staff, materials, equipment for COE)

5. **Governing Board Resolution**
   - Approved by all directors
   - Statement: "We resolve to apply for 80G registration for the Centre of Excellence's skill development activities."

### 6.3 Filing Process

1. **Collect all docs** (Weeks 1–2)
2. **Notarize MOA/AOA + bylaws** (₹500–1,000 at notary)
3. **File Form 10G online:**
   - Visit **https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in**
   - Portal: e-Services → Form 10G (online)
   - Upload PDF + supporting docs
4. **ITR office processes** (30–60 days)
5. **Approval letter issued** → forwarded via email
6. **80G certificate valid for 5 years** (renewable)

### 6.4 Benefits of 80G for Ashle Gardens COE

| Benefit | Impact |
|---|---|
| **Donor tax deduction** | Individuals donate ₹1L → claim ₹50K–100K tax deduction (depends on Income Tax category). Makes CSR pitches more attractive. |
| **Corporate CSR credibility** | CSR managers can cite 80G approval as proof of transparency. Easier to secure ₹25–50L CSR grants from Sivakasi fireworks, textile mills. |
| **Grant eligibility** | Most government skill development schemes (TN CM Kaushal Pattarai, TNSDC grants) require 80G status. |
| **International partnerships** | Combined with FCRA: qualifies for GIZ, Aga Khan, UNDP rural livelihood funding. |
| **Perpetual donor base** | Builds permanent funding stream (annual donations, recurring CSR commitments). |

# COE Philosophy — Ashle Gardens Centre of Excellence

> **Corrected vision (founder note, 2026-05-16)**

## What the COE is NOT

- NOT a paid apprenticeship program (no stipend-based worker model)
- NOT a vocational training centre running government schemes
- NOT a charity workforce pipeline

## What the COE IS

The Ashle Gardens COE is a **pro bono innovation residency** for brilliant young minds — engineering students, design graduates, research scholars, and young entrepreneurs — who want to build something real and useful for both society and commercial markets.

**The live problem set:** Ashle Gardens and its 10+ portfolio businesses provide the real-world challenges. The COE residents solve them. Their solutions become actual products, services, and systems running on the campus.

### The deal (pro bono model)
- Resident gets: **free stay on campus**, meals, tools, mentorship, a real problem, co-founders, and the right to commercialise their solution
- Ashle Gardens gets: **working solutions** built by people who care about the problem, not just a paycheck
- Society gets: **transferable knowledge** — every solution is open-sourced or published so other farms, laundries, kitchens, and retirement communities can use it

### Why this model works
1. **Intrinsic motivation beats extrinsic** — people who chose the problem build better solutions than people hired to solve it
2. **Live deployment** — no simulation. The biogas IoT monitor goes on the actual plant. The linen tracking runs on the actual laundry. Stakes are real.
3. **Portfolio for the builder** — "I built the water management system for a 40-acre circular economy campus" is far stronger than any internship certificate
4. **Network effect** — alumni from Ashle Gardens COE become brand ambassadors, referrers of the next cohort, and future investors/partners
5. **Research publications** — every solution generates data. Papers get published. Ashle Gardens becomes a cited living laboratory.

### Cohort structure
- **Duration:** 3–12 months (self-chosen based on project scope)
- **Cohort size:** 8–12 residents per cohort (intimate, high-quality)
- **Frequency:** 2 cohorts/year (Jan and July intakes)
- **Accommodation:** 8 capsule eco-houses dedicated to COE residents
- **Workspace:** COE Innovation Lab (1,500 sqft, shared with co-working)
- **Mentors:** Founder + each of the 10 specialist advisors as domain mentors + external industry mentors

### What Ashle Gardens commits to
- Free accommodation (capsule eco-house) for duration of project
- Meals from the Ashle kitchen (zero-mile food)
- Access to all campus infrastructure (lab, farm, laundry, kitchen, lake)
- ₹50,000 prototype budget per project (non-dilutive)
- IP stays with the builder; Ashle gets a perpetual royalty-free license to use on campus
- Ashle helps connect finished solutions to commercial markets (laundry to hospitals, biochar to farms, etc.)

### Revenue model for Ashle Gardens (indirect)
The COE does NOT charge residents. Revenue comes from:
1. **CSR sponsorships** — companies sponsor specific projects (₹5-25L per project)
2. **Research grants** — DST, ICMR, NABARD, ICAR for agricultural/health/food projects
3. **IP licensing** — if a solution is commercialised by the resident, Ashle gets 3-5% royalty from commercial sales outside campus
4. **Talent pipeline** — best COE alumni are offered to Ashle Gardens' B2B partners (hospitals, hotels) for placement fees
5. **Publication and reputation** — attracts premium guests, NRI investors, and eco-tourism visitors who want to see the innovation lab

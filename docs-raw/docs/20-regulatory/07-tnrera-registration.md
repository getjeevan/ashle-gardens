# TNRERA Registration — Ashle Gardens
*Tamil Nadu Real Estate Regulatory Authority | Prepared by Adv. Meenakshi*

## When TNRERA Registration is Mandatory
Under Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act 2016, Section 3:

**Registration REQUIRED if:**
- Built-up area of ALL phases COMBINED exceeds **500 sq metres**, OR
- Number of apartments/units in a project exceeds **8**

**Ashle Gardens:** With 30 heritage suites + 24 capsule eco-houses = 54 units → TNRERA mandatory before ANY advertising or advance collection.

⚠️ **Cannot advertise, market, book, or collect any advance without TNRERA registration**

## What to Register
Each distinct phase must be registered separately:
- Phase 1: First 8 heritage suites + 12 capsule houses (20 units)
- Phase 2: Next 12 suites + 8 capsule houses (20 units)
- Phase 3: Final 10 suites + 4 wellness suites (14 units)

Register Phase 1 at least 60 days before launch.

## Registration Portal
**URL:** [https://rera.tn.gov.in](https://rera.tn.gov.in)
**Form:** REG-1 (Promoter Registration)

## Documents Required

### Land documents
- Patta (all 3 patta numbers — 56, 113, 446) — from eServices TN
- Encumbrance Certificate (last 30 years)
- Land conversion order (Section 27/28 — must be obtained first)
- DTCP layout approval

### Project documents
- Approved building plan (from Kariapatti Panchayat or DTCP Virudhunagar)
- Structural safety certificate
- Environmental clearance (if applicable)
- TNPCB Consent to Establish (CTE)

### Financial documents
- CA certificate: balance in designated escrow account ≥ 70% of amount collected from allottees
- Audited balance sheet of promoter company (last 3 years or since incorporation)
- Income tax returns (last 3 years)

### Promoter documents
- Certificate of Incorporation (Ashle Gardens Pvt Ltd)
- PAN of company and directors
- Passport photos of directors

## The 70% Escrow Rule — Critical
TNRERA Section 4(2)(l)(D): **At least 70% of all amounts collected from buyers** must be deposited in a separate designated bank account. This can ONLY be used for:
- Land cost
- Construction cost

**The remaining 30% is freely usable for other purposes.**

**Practical implication:**
If you collect ₹1 Cr from buyers → ₹70L must stay in TNRERA escrow until construction milestones are certified by architect. Only released on percentage completion basis.

## Registration Fee
₹5 per sq ft of carpet area (capped at ₹10 lakhs for large projects)
For Phase 1 (approx 20,000 sqft carpet area): ₹1 lakh

## Timeline
- Application submission to TNRERA: Day 1
- Query resolution period: 15–30 days
- Registration granted: 30–60 days from application
- Registration valid: Until project completion certificate or 10 years (whichever earlier)

## Post-Registration Obligations
1. **Quarterly updates** — submit project completion % on TNRERA portal
2. **Annual audit** — CA audit of escrow account submitted to TNRERA
3. **Complaint redressal** — respond to buyer complaints within 45 days
4. **No changes without consent** — structural changes after registration need buyer consent + TNRERA approval

## Penalties for Non-Registration
- Penalty: **up to 10% of estimated project cost** (for not registering)
- Criminal prosecution: imprisonment up to 3 years for wilful violation
- Do NOT collect even a token advance without registration

## Practical Advice
1. Complete land conversion FIRST (Section 27/28) — TNRERA won't register on agricultural land
2. Get DTCP layout approval BEFORE TNRERA application — it's a required document
3. Open designated escrow account at a scheduled bank (SBI, Canara, Indian Bank) BEFORE filing
4. Don't launch any social media or brochure without RERA number printed on it

# NRI Buyer Handbook — Ashle Gardens
*Prepared by Adv. Meenakshi | Kariapatti, Virudhunagar*

## FEMA Rules for NRI Purchase

### What NRIs CAN buy (automatic route — no RBI permission needed)
- Residential property ✅ — our heritage suites and capsule eco-houses qualify
- Commercial property ✅ — co-working spaces qualify

### What NRIs CANNOT directly own
- Agricultural land ❌ — our farm land must be in Pvt Ltd (Indian majority) or resident Indian name
- Plantation land ❌
- Farmhouse ❌ (defined as agricultural land with residential structure)

**Our retirement units are residential — fully automatic route. No RBI permission needed.**

## Payment Rules
- Payment MUST come from NRE/NRO/FCNR account or foreign inward remittance
- Cannot pay in foreign currency cash or traveller's cheque
- Cheque from NRO account (funds already in India) is permitted

## Bank Accounts

| Account | Source | Use for purchase |
|---|---|---|
| NRE (Non-Resident External) | Salary/income earned abroad | ✅ Can pay from here; proceeds repatriable |
| NRO (Non-Resident Ordinary) | Income from India (rent, dividends) | ✅ Can pay; repatriation has ceiling |
| FCNR (Foreign Currency NR) | Foreign currency deposits | ✅ Can use; fully repatriable |

## TDS on Purchase (when seller is NRI)
If buyer purchases from an NRI seller:
- Buyer must deduct TDS at **20% + 4% surcharge = 20.8%** on sale value (NOT just capital gains)
- Deposit TDS via Form 26QB within 30 days
- Issue Form 16B to seller
- NRI seller can apply to AO for lower deduction certificate if actual gain is lower

*When buying from Alagiri Nayakkar / Silukka family (Indian residents), standard 1% TDS applies for value >₹50L.*

## Repatriation of Sale Proceeds
After selling the property in future:
- **NRE purchase**: proceeds fully repatriable abroad
- **NRO purchase**: up to **USD 1 million per financial year** repatriable (Form 15CA/15CB required)
- Maximum 2 residential properties can be repatriated in lifetime

## Power of Attorney (POA) for Absentee Buyers
If NRI cannot attend Sub-Registrar office in person:
- Execute a **specific POA** (not general) — name the specific property and transaction
- Get POA notarised AND apostilled in country of residence
- Attest at Indian Consulate/Embassy
- Register POA at Sub-Registrar office in Kariapatti/Virudhunagar
- POA holder executes registration on NRI's behalf

## Inheritance
- Under Hindu Succession Act 1956: NRI children/spouse inherit equally with resident Indians
- Inherited property can be repatriated (up to USD 1M/year) after paying applicable taxes
- Foreign heirs (non-Indian citizens) cannot inherit agricultural land — our residential units are fine

## GST on Ashle Gardens Purchase
- **Under-construction unit**: GST 5% (reduced from 12% under affordable housing scheme — check eligibility)
- **Ready / completed unit** (OC received): NO GST
- **Resale**: NO GST
- Strategy: take possession only after OC to avoid GST

## Practical Checklist for NRI Buyer
- [ ] Open NRE/NRO account if not already done
- [ ] Obtain PAN card (mandatory for property purchase)
- [ ] Execute specific POA if attending in person is not possible
- [ ] Ensure payment comes from NRE/NRO — no cash
- [ ] Verify Ashle Gardens Pvt Ltd has TNRERA registration before paying any advance
- [ ] Check for 70% escrow compliance (TNRERA requirement)
- [ ] Get loan pre-approval from SBI NRI branch if financing

*Reference: FEMA 1999, Section 6(5); RBI Master Circular on Acquisition of Immovable Property in India*

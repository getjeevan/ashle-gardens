## PHASE 0 — Before Construction Starts

### 🔴 1. DIC Virudhunagar — MSME Registration
**Expected Benefit:** ₹3.5+ Crore cumulative (infrastructure + interest + employment subsidies)  
**Prerequisite:** Company incorporation certificate + patta in company name

**Documents to gather:**
- [ ] Company registration certificate (ROC)
- [ ] Patta (transferred & registered in company name)
- [ ] Business plan (project description, investment, timeline, employment plan)
- [ ] Bank statement showing initial capital
- [ ] Utility connection copy (water, power) — can be preliminary

**Action steps:**
- [ ] Visit DIC Virudhunagar in person (Near Collectorate, Virudhunagar | +91-451-2471234 | dic.vrd@tn.gov.in)
- [ ] Submit registration packet — DIC Registration Certificate issued in **10–15 days**
- [ ] File separately for Infrastructure Subsidy (15–20% of fixed assets, cap ₹50L) — **20–30 days**
- [ ] File for Interest Subsidy (4–5% on term loans, cap ₹1 Cr loan) — **20–30 days**
- [ ] File for Employment Subsidy (₹15–25K per local hire, cap 100 employees) — after hiring begins

**Notes:**
- Hire 50%+ staff from Virudhunagar district to qualify for employment subsidy
- Each subsidy requires a separate application; budget 3–4 months to secure all
- Property tax exemption (50–100% for 5 years) also unlocked via DIC certificate — submit to Panchayat post-operational

---

### 🟠 2. NPHCE — National Programme for Health Care of the Elderly
**Expected Benefit:** ₹1–2 Crore (5-year program: infrastructure grant + recurring health subsidy + training grant)  
**Prerequisite:** Layout approval + building plan approval (need clinic design on record)  
**Ministry:** Social Justice & Empowerment (MoSJE) | nphce@msje.gov.in | +91-11-2338-5000

**Documents to gather:**
- [ ] Layout approval order
- [ ] Building plan showing geriatric clinic, physiotherapy wing, diagnostic area
- [ ] Geriatric care staffing plan (geriatrician, nurses, physiotherapists)
- [ ] 5-year financial plan for the health facility
- [ ] BPL commitment affidavit (30% of residents served at subsidized rates)
- [ ] MoU with local hospital/medical board for clinical oversight
- [ ] Land ownership proof (patta)

**Action steps:**
- [ ] Register with **MoSJE National Center for Aging**: https://ncage.gov.in/
- [ ] Prepare 15-page proposal (facility design, staffing plan, financial plan, BPL commitment)
- [ ] Submit to **Regional Director, MoSJE South Zone, Chennai** (+91-44-2849-4580 | nphce.south@msje.gov.in)
- [ ] MoSJE site visit (15–30 days after submission)
- [ ] Approval letter (30–45 days) → **Total: 60–90 days from submission**
- [ ] First grant tranche disbursed post-construction (6–12 months later)

**Notes:**
- Virudhunagar District qualifies — only one geriatric facility in the district
- BPL commitment (30% subsidized) is binding; design fee structure so premium units cross-subsidize
- Training grant (₹10–20L) requires annual geriatric care assistant training program — can be revenue-generating

### 🟡 3. Guidance TN — Single-Window Fast-Track (if ₹100 Cr+ project)
**Expected Benefit:** Regulatory fast-track (120 days vs 9–12 months scattered approvals)  
**Prerequisite:** Total project investment confirmed at ₹100 Cr+  
**Department:** TN Industrial Guidance Bureau | invest@tn.gov.in | +91-44-2825-7000

**Documents to gather:**
- [ ] Investment proposal (15–20 pages: description, location, investment, timeline, employment, sustainability)
- [ ] Company incorporation certificate
- [ ] Patta + EC
- [ ] Preliminary layout plan
- [ ] Financial statements (proof of promoter capacity)
- [ ] Environmental sustainability plan

**Action steps:**
- [ ] Confirm total investment figure (land + construction + infra + soft costs)
- [ ] Submit via https://www.investingintamilnadu.com/mega-project-portal/
- [ ] Guidance Bureau review (15–30 days) → Dedicated nodal officer assigned
- [ ] Nodal officer coordinates all state approvals → **Final approval in 120 days**

**Notes:**
- Only pursue if total investment is ₹100 Cr or more; otherwise skip
- Does not waive requirements — consolidates handoffs to one officer

## PHASE 1 — During Layout & Building Plan (Months 4–6)

### 🟠 4. Swadesh Darshan 2.0 — Heritage & Wellness Tourism Grant
**Expected Benefit:** ₹1–3 Crore (50–75% subsidy on common infrastructure, cap ₹5 Cr)  
**Prerequisite:** TNRERA registration + TTDC circuit alignment letter  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Tourism | sd@tourism.gov.in | +91-11-2371-1000

**Documents to gather:**
- [ ] TNRERA registration certificate
- [ ] Sustainable Tourism Master Plan (STMP) — 30–40 page document including:
  - [ ] Heritage/wellness USP + connection to Meenakshi-Meghamalai tourism circuit
  - [ ] Infrastructure details (accommodations, amenities, waste management)
  - [ ] Employment & community benefit plan (50%+ local hiring)
  - [ ] 10-year projection (revenue, occupancy, jobs)
- [ ] Tourism circuit alignment letter from **TTDC** (Tamil Nadu Tourism Development Corporation)
- [ ] Bank guarantee for grant refund (if occupancy <70% in Yr 1–2)
- [ ] Employment plan (local hiring commitment)
- [ ] Waste management & sustainability plan

**Action steps:**
- [ ] Engage TTDC Madurai (+91-452-2348101) — obtain circuit alignment letter for Meenakshi–Meghamalai heritage corridor
- [ ] Prepare STMP with architect + consultant
- [ ] Submit to Ministry of Tourism via TN Tourism Dept (state nodal agency)
- [ ] Ministry feasibility study (45–60 days)
- [ ] Approval letter + grant-in-aid agreement signed → **Total: 90–120 days**
- [ ] Grant disbursed in tranches post-construction

**Notes:**
- Circuit alignment is critical — Meenakshipuram → Meghamalai → Sivagangai is the strongest narrative
- Bank guarantee risk: if occupancy <70% in Yr 1, partial refund may be required
- Covers common infra: gardens, wellness centre, dining hall, entry gate — not individual units

### 🟠 6. NABARD — Farm + Biogas Subsidies
**Expected Benefit:** ₹6.3L (DEDS dairy + biogas) + up to ₹50L at 6.5% (RIDF farm infra)  
**Prerequisite:** Farm design finalized; biogas plant specifications ready  
**Contact:** NABARD District Development Manager, Virudhunagar | Apply via Bank of India/SBI agri branch

**DEDS (Dairy Entrepreneurship Development Scheme):**
- [ ] Confirm cow shed + biogas configuration (6 cows = ₹3.3L + ₹3L = ₹6.3L subsidy)
- [ ] Apply through Bank of India or SBI agri branch with cow shed design + biogas specs
- [ ] NABARD DDM processes in **3–4 months**

**RIDF (Rural Infrastructure Development Fund):**
- [ ] Prepare farm infrastructure plan (irrigation, storage, internal roads)
- [ ] Apply for up to ₹50L at 6.5% via NABARD DDM
- [ ] Contact: NABARD Virudhunagar: through SBI/Bank of India agri branch

## PHASE 2 — Post-TNRERA, Pre-Operations (Months 9–12)

### 🟠 8. SAATHI — Tourism Digital Registration
**Expected Benefit:** ₹1–10L (one-time) + credit access at 7–8% vs 12–14% market rate  
**Prerequisite:** Operational (even partially) with at least 5 units running  
**Ministry:** Ministry of Tourism | saathi@tourism.gov.in | +91-11-2371-1000

**Action steps:**
- [ ] Register on SAATHI app: https://saathi.tourism.gov.in/
- [ ] Upload property details, photos, unit count
- [ ] Ministry verifies within **15–30 days**
- [ ] Certification issued → credit scheme access enabled immediately

**Notes:**
- Lowest effort, highest credit access benefit
- No ongoing compliance burden
- Do this on Day 1 of operations

**Prerequisite:** Udyam registration + 3 months of GST returns + signed SLA contracts (hospital, laundry clients)  
**Contact:** DIC Virudhunagar + Canara Bank or Indian Bank (DIC tie-up branch)

- [ ] Hospital/laundry SLA contracts (proof of revenue pipeline)

- [ ] DIC MSME registration certificate (from Step 1 above)

**Action steps:**
- [ ] Apply at DIC Virudhunagar + preferred lender (Canara/Indian Bank)
- [ ] Processing: **6–8 weeks**

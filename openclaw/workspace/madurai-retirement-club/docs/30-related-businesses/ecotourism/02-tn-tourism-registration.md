## 1. HERITAGE HOMESTAY CLASSIFICATION

### Why This Matters
**Heritage Homestay** status from TN Tourism gives legal recognition, GST exemption eligibility, and marketing advantage. Ashle Gardens' Chettinad courtyard rooms fit perfectly.

### Eligibility Criteria
- ✅ **Architecture:** 50+ years old or authentic heritage design → Chettinad courtyard building qualifies
- ✅ **Capacity:** 4–20 guest rooms → Ashle has 6–8 heritage rooms (suitable range)
- ✅ **Character:** Retains original heritage elements (lime mortar, wooden pillars, courtyards) → Yes
- ✅ **Food:** Serves traditional cuisine → Chettinad cooking planned
- ✅ **Experience:** Authentic living, not museum experience → Core to our model

### Application Process

#### Step 1: Pre-Application Documentation (Weeks 1–2)
Gather these documents:
- **Property Ownership:** Title deed / sale agreement (scan colored copies)
- **Heritage Value Statement:** 500 words on building history, architectural significance, why it matters to Madurai heritage
- **Floor Plan:** Building layout (CAD or detailed sketch) showing guest areas, common areas, kitchen
- **Photo Album:** 15–20 photos of exterior, courtyard, rooms, architectural details, kitchen
- **Guest Experience Documentation:** Sample daily itinerary, meals, activities, sensory storytelling angles
- **Staff Credentials:** List hospitality staff + educational background (important for homestay credibility)
- **Sustainability Practices:** Water conservation, waste management, energy use (homestays are eco-certified)

**Responsibility:** Deepa + Venue Coordinator + Photographer

**Timeline:** Complete by June 15, 2026

---

#### Step 2: Application to TN Tourism Department
**Contact:** TN Tourism Development Corporation (TTDC) Madurai Office  
**Address:** TTDC Hotel Tamil Nadu, 101 W Veli Street, Madurai 625 001  
**Phone:** +91-452-2343-4111

1. **Fill Form:** TTDC Heritage Homestay Application (available at office or website)

4. **Fees:** ₹5,000 (non-refundable; valid 2 years)

#### Step 3: Inspection & Approval
**TN Tourism Inspector visits** to verify:
- Heritage authenticity
- Room standards (cleanliness, safety, basic amenities)
- Kitchen hygiene

**Prepare:** Schedule 2–3 hours, ensure rooms are photo-ready, have staff briefed on Q&A

**Timeline:** 1–2 weeks after submission (schedule with inspector)

---

#### Step 4: Certification
**Outcome:** 
- **Approval:** Certificate of Recognition as Heritage Homestay (valid 5 years)
- **Benefits:**
  - Listing in TTDC Heritage Homestay directory (online + printed)
  - ₹50,000–100,000 annual subsidy potential (if eligible for MICE/experiential tier)
  - Tax exemption on room rental (if structured as homestay vs. hotel)
  - Priority access to TN Tourism promotion events

**Timeline:** 2–4 weeks after inspection

---

### GST Compliance (Heritage Homestay)
**Important:** If classified as Heritage Homestay, GST treatment changes:
- **GST Rate:** 5% (vs. 12% for regular hotels) on room charges only
- **Food Service:** Still 5% if bundled with accommodation; standalone meals are 5% veg, 5% non-veg
- **ITC:** No input tax credit (typically, check with CA)

**Action:** Coordinate with Ashle Gardens' CA for GST registration update; file amended Form GSTR-7 if needed.

**Timeline:** Immediately after Heritage Homestay approval

## 2. ECO-TOURISM OPERATOR REGISTRATION (TN TOURISM)

### Why This Matters
Mandatory registration for any operator offering eco-tourism experiences in Tamil Nadu. Opens B2B doors (government partnerships, school relationships, CSR contracts).

### Eligibility
- ✅ Registered business entity (Ashle Gardens PvtLtd) → Yes
- ✅ Eco-tourism focus (conservation, sustainability, education) → Yes (biochar, permaculture, renewable energy)
- ✅ Minimum 3 experiences offered → Yes (12 programs designed)
- ✅ Environmental management plan → To prepare
- ✅ Staff training credentials → To develop

### Application Process

#### Step 1: Environmental Management Plan (Weeks 1–3)
**Document:** 10–15 page write-up on how Ashle Gardens' eco-tourism minimizes environmental impact:

**Include:**
1. **Waste Management:** Plastic-free operations, composting, recycling systems
2. **Water Conservation:** Rainwater harvesting (% of campus water use), wastewater treatment
3. **Energy Use:** Renewable energy sources (solar, biogas), energy audit
4. **Wildlife & Biodiversity:** Native tree planting (target count), bird sanctuary protocols, wildlife awareness in programs
5. **Community Benefit:** % of revenue to local communities, student employment, agricultural partnership
6. **Carrying Capacity:** Max daily visitors (calculated based on campus area, environmental load)
7. **Monitoring & Reporting:** How impact is measured quarterly

**Template Source:** Download from TN Tourism website or contact TTDC

**Responsibility:** Deepa + Sustainability Lead + Operations Manager

**Draft by:** June 1, 2026; Final by June 15, 2026

## 5. SWADESH DARSHAN 2.0 GRANT ELIGIBILITY

### Program Overview
Central government (Ministry of Tourism) funds infrastructure development for heritage destinations under **Swadesh Darshan 2.0**. Madurai is in Tier-1 heritage circuits.

### How It Works
- **Eligible Projects:** Heritage site development, eco-tourism infrastructure, community homestays, signage, visitor facilities
- **Funding:** Up to ₹50 crores for major heritage circuit projects
- **Ashle Gardens Role:** Can be **sub-component** of larger Madurai heritage circuit project

### Ashle Gardens' Potential Projects for Grant

#### Project 1: Heritage Courtyard Restoration & Homestay Facility
- **Investment:** ₹10–15 lakhs (restoration + guest facilities)
- **Grant Coverage:** Up to 60% (₹6–9 lakhs)
- **Components:** Courtyard restoration, guest rooms, kitchen upgrade, heritage interpretation center

#### Project 2: Eco-tourism Visitor Experience Center
- **Investment:** ₹8–12 lakhs (interpretive center, signage, seating areas, biochar lab public space)
- **Grant Coverage:** Up to 60% (₹5–7 lakhs)
- **Components:** Permanent displays (Madurai heritage, biochar science, permaculture), guided experience areas

#### Project 3: Community Participation Program
- **Investment:** ₹3–5 lakhs (train local guides, develop school programs, COE student scholarships)
- **Grant Coverage:** Up to 70% for community benefit component (₹2–3 lakhs)

### Application Process

#### Step 1: Eligibility & LOI (Letters of Intent) — Months 1–2
- Contact **TN Tourism Department, State Nodal Agency (SNA)** for Swadesh Darshan 2.0
- **Email:** sna@tntodc.tn.gov.in
- **Phone:** +91-44-2846-7777
- **Submit:** 2–3 page LOI describing project, budget, Ashle Gardens' role, expected community benefit

#### Step 2: Detailed Project Report (DPR) — Months 2–4
If LOI approved, prepare full DPR:
- **Contents:**
  1. Executive summary
  2. Project justification (Madurai heritage importance, visitor demand, gap analysis)
  3. Technical scope (architecture, layouts, facilities, sustainability features)
  4. Detailed cost estimate (itemized budget)
  5. Implementation timeline (6–12 months typical)
  6. Community benefit plan (local employment, training, revenue-sharing)
  7. Environmental & social impact assessment
  8. Financial viability (projected revenue post-completion)

**Responsible:** Deepa + Architect + Finance Lead

#### Step 3: State-Level Approval — Months 4–6
- DPR reviewed by TN Tourism Department and Central Ministry of Tourism
- Possible site visit by Ministry officials
- Approval or request for modifications

#### Step 4: Grant Disbursement — Months 6–18
- Project execution with contractor
- Quarterly monitoring by TN Tourism
- Grant paid in tranches (typically 30-40-30 schedule)

## 6. GREEN CERTIFICATIONS (OPTIONAL, HIGH-IMPACT)

These are not mandatory but highly marketable for international eco-tourists and corporate CSR programs.

### Option A: ECOTOURISM India Label (Ministry of Tourism)
- **What:** Certifies bona fide eco-tourism operators meeting environmental + social standards
- **Duration:** 1-year process
- **Cost:** ₹30,000–50,000
- **Benefits:** International marketing credibility, 10–15% higher pricing justifiable
- **Typical Timeline:** Complete after Eco-tourism Operator registration (follow-up)

### Option B: Green Globe Certification (International)
- **What:** Global eco-tourism certification (Europe-based but accepted worldwide)
- **Duration:** 3–4 months (post-audit process)
- **Cost:** ₹1.5–2.5 lakhs (annual)
- **Benefits:** Premium market positioning, appeals to international guests + corporate sustainability reports
- **Realistic for Ashle:** Apply end of 2026 or 2027 (after proving operations)

### Option C: EarthCheck Certification (Australia-based)
- **What:** Environmental footprint verification (water, waste, carbon, biodiversity)
- **Duration:** 2–3 months
- **Cost:** ₹1–1.5 lakhs
- **Benefits:** Strong for corporate sustainability messaging

### Recommendation for Ashle Gardens
**2026 Priority:** Skip international certifications; focus on government registrations (Heritage Homestay + Eco-tourism Operator). These build credibility fast and unlock government support.

**2027+ Plan:** Pursue ECOTOURISM India label (lower cost, strong local-international credibility mix). Green Globe/EarthCheck if international tourism scales.

# Ashle Gardens Experience Catalogue
## Eco-tourism & Experiential Programs 2025–26

**Director:** Deepa | **Last Updated:** 2026-05-16

---

## Overview

Ashle Gardens offers curated experiences across four pillars:
1. **Day Visits** (3–6 hrs) — Heritage, farm, craft, environmental education
2. **Overnight Packages** (24–48 hrs) — Immersive stays with sensory storytelling
3. **MICE Programs** (team-building, CSR, offsite) — Corporate, educational, CSR
4. **School Programs** (4–6 hrs) — Curriculum-linked visits for CBSE/ICSE schools

All programs integrate **COE (Centre of Excellence) student involvement** — mentoring, research support, culinary partnership, and income generation.

## Overview: Kitchen Zones & Flow

The central kitchen follows **linear flow design** (contamination prevention):
1. **Receiving** → 2. **Cold Storage** → 3. **Dry Prep** → 4. **Wet Prep** → 5. **Cooking** → 6. **Roti/Bread** → 7. **Plating & Dispatch** → 8. **Wash Area**

**Total Kitchen Built-up Area:** 2,500 sq ft (232 sq m)

## Safety & Compliance Checklist

- [ ] Kitchen layout approved by local municipal corporation
- [ ] Electrical installation certified by licensed electrician
- [ ] Fire suppression system installed & certified annually
- [ ] Water supply tested by ICMR-certified lab (quarterly)
- [ ] All equipment food-contact surfaces: stainless steel 304/316
- [ ] Temperature monitoring: cold storage (digital logger), cooking (meat thermometer), hot holding (Bain Marie thermostat)
- [ ] Exhaust ductwork cleaned monthly; filter replaced quarterly
- [ ] Pest control contract active (monthly visits)
- [ ] Staff trained: FSSAI Food Handler cert for all 5+ staff

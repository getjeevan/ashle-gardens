## CATERING MENU — Heritage Events

### Wedding / Family Function (veg Saiva menu)
- Breakfast: Idli, Dosa, Pongal, Upma station
- Lunch (20-item): Full Saiva thali — rice + 4 kuzhambu + 4 poriyal + rasam + sambar + mor + kootu + pachadi + papad + pickle + payasam + appam + vadai
- Price: ₹350–500/plate (depends on occasion)

### Non-veg special (corporate events, NRI family dinners)
- Starters: Mutton seekh kebab, Nandu fry, Meen varuval
- Main: Nattu kozhi kari, Mutton Chettinad, Jigarthanda dessert
- Price: ₹550–750/plate

### Jigarthanda (signature Madurai dessert)
- **Tamil:** ஜிகர்தண்டா
- Cold milk-based dessert with badam pisin, nannari syrup, ice cream
- Available as standalone item for guests/visitors: ₹80–120/glass
- Can be sold at farm tourism visits too

## Nutritional Notes for Therapeutic Diets

| Condition | Modifications |
|---|---|
| Diabetes | Brown rice, no sugar, low-glycaemic vegetables, reduce rice portion |
| Hypertension | Low sodium — no added salt in cooking; natural spice flavouring |
| Renal (CKD) | Low potassium — avoid banana, tomato, potato; limit protein |
| Post-surgical soft | Kanji (rice gruel) + boiled vegetables, no spice |
| Jain | No root vegetables (no onion, garlic, potato, carrot) |

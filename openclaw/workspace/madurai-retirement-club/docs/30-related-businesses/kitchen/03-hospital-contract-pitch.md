## The Problem (For Hospital Administrators)

Running a hospital dietary department in-house is expensive, wasteful, and audit-risky:

| Pain point | Typical Madurai hospital |
|---|---|
| In-house cost per meal | ₹40–75 (including staff, kitchen, wastage) |
| Food wastage | 25–35% |
| NABH dietary compliance | Often poorly documented (FS.4/FS.5 gaps) |
| Kitchen staff turnover | High (30–40%/year in hospital sector) |
| Menu consistency | Variable by shift and chef |
| Therapeutic diet accuracy | Inconsistent — relies on individual cook |

**For a 300-bed hospital serving 900 meals/day:** in-house cost = ₹36,000–67,500/day = ₹1.3–2.5 Cr/year

## The Ashle Gardens Solution

A FSSAI-licensed central kitchen, HACCP-certified, nutritionist-curated, GPS-tracked delivery.

**What you get:**
- Daily delivery of hot meals in insulated containers (temperature maintained 63°C+ for hot, 4°C for cold)
- 2 delivery rounds per day (morning + afternoon/evening)
- WhatsApp photo proof of each meal batch before dispatch
- Monthly menu calendar (30-day rotation) signed off by your Dietary Head
- NABH FS.4 and FS.5 documentation kit provided monthly
- Dedicated relationship manager (direct mobile — 24/7 for emergencies)

## Quality Assurance

- **HACCP:** All 7 principles implemented. CCP logs available for audit.
- **Temperature log:** Auto-recorded by food warmers. Provided monthly.
- **NABH documentation:** FS.4 (Dietary Management) + FS.5 (Food Safety) compliance kit — we prepare it for your NABH renewal
- **Microbiological testing:** Quarterly third-party lab test on food samples (Staphylococcus, E.coli, total count)
- **Nutritionist sign-off:** All therapeutic diets reviewed by a registered dietitian

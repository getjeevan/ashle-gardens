## Part 1: FSSAI License Type Selection

### Why Central License?

| Criteria | State License | Central License |
|----------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Turnover threshold** | ₹12 Cr or less | >₹12 Cr OR multi-state supply |
| **Our trigger** | No | YES (hospital contracts + cloud kitchen) |
| **Annual fee** | ₹500–2,000 | ₹7,500 |
| **Validity** | 2 years | 1 year |
| **Hospital contracts** | Limited | Full compliance recognized nationwide |
| **Multi-state catering** | Restricted | Permitted with NOC |

**Decision:** FSSAI Central License (valid nationally, required for hospital dietary compliance)

## Part 3: HACCP Plan – Core Framework

**HACCP** (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points) is mandatory for Central License.

### HACCP Principles (7-step framework)

| Step | Focus | Example for Ashle Kitchen |
|------|-------|---------------------------|
| 1. **Hazard Analysis** | Identify food safety risks | Raw chicken contamination, allergens (sesame oil), thermal processing failure |
| 2. **CCP Identification** | Critical control points | Chicken storage (4°C), cooking temp (75°C), hot holding (65°C) |
| 3. **Critical Limits** | Set safe thresholds | Raw chicken: ≤4°C; cooked meat: ≥75°C; held food: 60–65°C |
| 4. **Monitoring** | Check CCP compliance | Temperature logs (daily), visual checks (color, odor) |
| 5. **Corrective Action** | React to failures | Chicken >5°C → discard; underooked meat → reboil |
| 6. **Verification** | Audit CCP systems | Weekly manager review of logs, quarterly external audit |
| 7. **Records** | Document everything | Temperature chart, corrective action log, staff training records |

### HACCP Procedures for Ashle Kitchen

#### **CCP 1: Cold Storage (Receiving)**
- **Hazard:** Bacterial growth (Salmonella, Listeria) if temp >4°C
- **Critical Limit:** ≤4°C
- **Monitoring:** Temperature probe daily (9 AM), log on chart
- **Action if exceeded:** Check thermostat, call technician; quarantine affected items
- **Records:** Cold storage log (visible on wall)

#### **CCP 2: Chicken/Meat Cooking**
- **Hazard:** Pathogens (E. coli, Salmonella) if undercooked
- **Critical Limit:** Internal temp ≥75°C (measured with calibrated thermometer)
- **Monitoring:** Chef checks with meat thermometer before plating
- **Action if failed:** Return to heat, recheck
- **Records:** Cooking log (batch #, temp, time, chef initials)

#### **CCP 3: Hot Holding (Bain Marie)**
- **Hazard:** Pathogenic growth if food cools <60°C
- **Critical Limit:** 60–65°C
- **Monitoring:** Temperature check every 2 hours
- **Action if below:** Reheat to 75°C or discard
- **Records:** Hot holding log

#### **CCP 4: Allergen Control (Hospital Dietary Contracts)**
- **Hazard:** Sesame, peanut, shellfish contamination; cross-contact risk
- **Critical Limit:** Separate prep surface for allergen-free meals
- **Monitoring:** Staff briefing before each shift (allergen meal prep log)
- **Action if breach:** Quarantine batch, notify hospital
- **Records:** Allergen incident log

## Part 4: Hospital Dietary Compliance Add-Ons

### Additional Requirements for Hospital Supply Contracts

#### **1. NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals) Dietary Standards**

If supplying NABH-accredited hospitals (e.g., Meenakshi Hospital):

**Mandatory additions to HACCP:**
- **Therapeutic diet protocols:** Separate prep for diabetic, renal, cardiac meals
  - Example: Low-sodium kari kozhambu (skip added salt, use herbs for flavor)
  - Diabetic khichdi (millet + vegetables, no sugar)
  - Renal curry (minimal potassium vegetables: bottle gourd instead of tomato)

- **Nutrition labeling:** Every meal batch labeled with:
  - Calories, protein (g), carbs (g), fat (g), fiber (g), sodium (mg)
  - Example: Sambar + rice = 450 kcal, 12g protein

- **Hygiene certification:** Additional health certificate from district health officer (annual)

#### **2. Allergen Segregation (Hospital Requirement)**

**Dedicated prep zone for allergen-free meals:**
- Separate cutting board, utensils, gloves
- Clean prep table (no cross-contact with main kitchen)
- Staff training: 15-min daily briefing on allergen protocols
- Record: Allergen meal batch log (date, patient ID, meal type, staff sign-off)

#### **3. Water Quality Certification**

- **Quarterly test:** Ashle kitchen water → approved testing lab
- **Pathogenic bacteria test:** E. coli, coliforms must be zero
- **Cost:** ₹2,000–₹3,000 per test
- **Timeline:** Results in 3–5 days
- **Record:** Display certificate on kitchen wall; archive annually

#### **4. Pest Control Contract**

- **Monthly visits:** Licensed pest control contractor
- **Scope:** Rodent traps, cockroach spray, fly screens
- **Cost:** ₹2,500–₹3,500/month
- **Record:** Pest control log (date, type, areas sprayed, contractor sign-off)

#### **5. Staff Training**

- **Minimum 3 staff** hold FSSAI Food Safety Supervisor certificate (₹5,000–₹8,000 per person, 3-day course)
- **Annual refresher:** 4 hours on new hospital dietary standards
- **Certificate display:** Training certs visible on kitchen notice board

# Pet Spa & Hostel — Ashle Gardens (BU 12)

> **Added: 2026-05-16 | Founder directive**

## Concept

A **premium pet wellness and boarding facility** set within the Ashle Gardens eco-campus — giving pets the same heritage-calm environment their owners enjoy.

Madurai's urban pet ownership is growing rapidly (dog + cat ownership up 40% in 3 years in Tier-2 Tamil Nadu cities). No premium pet spa or farm-based pet hostel exists within 30 km. The campus — with open land, a lake, walking trails, and a farm — is a natural setting for pets to thrive while their owners travel or stay on-site.

**Tagline:** *Your pet deserves a retreat too.*

## Facility Design

### Pet Spa Wing (600 sqft)
- 3 grooming stations (hydraulic tables, professional dryers)
- 1 Ayurveda treatment bay (herbal soak tub, steam cabin)
- Waiting area with pet-viewing window (owners watch treatment)
- Storage for grooming supplies, herbal preparations
- Adjacent to Ayurveda spa building

### Pet Hostel (1,500 sqft + outdoor)
- 20 indoor kennels (10 small, 6 medium, 4 large)
- 4 premium suites (A/C, individual room, couch, TV playing nature sounds)
- 4 cat condos (vertical, multi-level, separate from dogs)
- Outdoor play yard: 2,000 sqft, artificial turf + shade net
- Separate quarantine room (for new arrivals, illness)
- Small animal section (rabbits, birds, hamsters): 6 units

### Pet-friendly capsule zone
- 4 of 24 capsule eco-houses with attached outdoor kennel (30 sqft)
- Easy-clean tile floors, pet-proof furniture
- Direct access to fenced farm-walk trail

## Regulatory
- **CPCSEA registration** (Committee for the Purpose of Control & Supervision of Experiments on Animals) — not needed since this is boarding, not research
- **PFA Act compliance** (Prevention of Cruelty to Animals) — standard kennelling conditions
- **Local panchayat NOC** — for kennel operations in Kariapatti panchayat
- **FSSAI** — if selling pet food prepared on campus
- **Veterinary tie-up** — formal MoU with a registered vet in Kariapatti/Tirumangalam for health checks and emergency calls

| **Dosing systems (chemicals for ETP)** | Automated; anionic polymer, pH control | 0.8 L |
| **Monitoring (pH, conductivity, COD probes)** | Real-time sensors + data logging | 1.2 L |
| **Piping, manifolds, valves** | PVC + stainless steel | 1 L |
| **Installation & testing (3-week commissioning)** | Labour + compliance certification | 1 L |
| **TOTAL WATER RECYCLING SYSTEM** | | **₹15–18 L** |

**Water Balance (2000 kg/day operation):**
- Fresh water input: 40,000 L/day (20 L/kg × 2000 kg)
- After first wash: 20,000 L to treatment
- RO recovery: 18,000 L (90% of 20,000 L)
- ETP polishing: 18,000 L → 14,000 L reusable
- Evaporation/loss: 2,000 L
- **Fresh water needed (makeup): 26,000 L → only 65% vs 100% industry baseline**

**Compliance value:**
- TNPCB Consent requirement: ≥60% recycle (we achieve 70%)
- ISO 14001: Water reduction metric (supports eco-certification)

## SIVAKASI INDUSTRIAL WORKWEAR MARKET

### Fireworks & Chemical Manufacturing (Sivakasi Industrial Belt)

**Key assumptions:**
- 300+ registered DGFT-licensed fireworks factories
- 50–100 workers per factory (avg 50)
- Workwear per worker: 2 uniforms + 1 safety coat = 3 pieces/worker
- Wash frequency: 2× per week
- Weight per piece: ~200g (avg uniform weight)

**Calculation:**
- 300 factories × 50 workers × 3 pieces × 0.2 kg = 9,000 kg/month across all factories
- Per factory: 30 kg/month → **~1.4 kg/week per factory**
- Total weekly across 300: 420 kg
- Daily: **60 kg/day (based on typical 2×/week wash schedule)**

## MARKET CAPTURE STRATEGY (Ashle Gardens Year 1–3)

### Competitive Landscape
- **Sri Sai Laundry** (Gandhi Nagar, Madurai): 800 kg/day, hotels only, weak compliance
- **Dhanalakshmi Laundry** (Avarampet): 600 kg/day, price-focused, no RFID
- **Apollo In-house laundry:** Self-serving only; not open to external B2B
- **Unorganized small operators:** <100 kg/day each, minimal equipment

**Fragmentation opportunity:** Most competitors lack NABH certification + RFID + eco-compliance. Ashle Gardens can differentiate.

### Year 1 Target: 12–15% TAM capture = 890–1,110 kg/day
- Hospital segment: 8–12 facilities × avg 20 kg/day = 160–240 kg
- Hotel segment: 4–6 properties × avg 25 kg/day = 100–150 kg
- Industrial segment: 20–30 small units × avg 2.5 kg/day = 50–75 kg
- **Y1 Total: 310–465 kg/day**
- **Y1 Revenue: ₹22.6–33.9 L**

### Year 2 Target: 25–30% TAM capture
- Hospital segment: 18–22 facilities = 360–440 kg
- Hotel segment: 10–12 properties = 250–300 kg
- Industrial segment: 60–80 units = 150–200 kg
- **Y2 Total: 760–940 kg/day**
- **Y2 Revenue: ₹55.4–68.5 L**

### Year 3 Target: 38–42% TAM capture
- Hospital segment: 28–35 facilities = 560–700 kg
- Hotel segment: 16–20 properties = 400–500 kg
- Industrial segment: 100–150 units = 250–375 kg
- **Y3 Total: 1,210–1,575 kg/day**
- **Y3 Revenue: ₹88–1.15 Cr**

**By Year 3, Ashle Gardens would operate at near-capacity (2000 kg/day facility), requiring expansion planning.**

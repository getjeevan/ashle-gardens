# Laundry Compliance Checklist — Ashle Gardens Commercial Laundry
*Anbu, Laundry Head | NABH · TNPCB · ISO 14001 · Fire NOC*

---

## Section 1 — NABH Standard 4.5: Linen Management

### Wash Temperature Protocol
- **Infection control minimum:** 71°C for minimum 3 minutes continuous
- **Alternative:** 65°C for minimum 10 minutes (thermally labile fabrics)
- **Documentation:** Auto-logging thermometer on each washer; daily log printed and signed by supervisor

### Colour-Coded Linen Bag System
| Bag colour | Linen category | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| White/Clear | Clean, processed linen | Trolley sealed, not opened in dirty zone |
| Yellow | Soiled, non-infected | Standard wash cycle |
| Red | Infected / barrier-nursed patients | Separate machine — algicidal pre-wash |
| Black | Contaminated / hazardous | Destroy or autoclave first |

### Traffic Flow (Zero Cross-Contamination)
- **Dirty side** (receiving, sorting): separate entry, dedicated trolleys
- **Clean side** (folding, packaging, dispatch): separate exit, no access from dirty side
- Staff must change gloves/aprons when crossing zones
- Trolleys: colour-coded — red for dirty, white for clean

### RFID Linen Tracking
- Each item tagged with RFID/barcode chip (survives 200+ washes)
- System records: item ID, fabric type, wash date/time, temperature, wash count
- Alert at 150 washes: item flagged for quality inspection
- Monthly loss rate target: < 2% of inventory
- Report provided to hospital Linen Management team monthly

### Microbiological Standards (NABH requirement)
Test quarterly using contact plates:
| Organism | Acceptable limit |
|---|---|
| Total aerobic count | < 10 CFU / 100 cm² |
| Staphylococcus aureus | 0 CFU detected |
| E. coli | 0 CFU detected |

Send to accredited lab (SGS India, Chennai or NABL lab in Madurai)

### Documentation for NABH Audit
- [ ] Daily wash temperature logs (auto-generated, supervisor-signed)
- [ ] Monthly linen count + loss report
- [ ] Quarterly microbiological test reports
- [ ] Chemical batch records (supplier certificate + dosing log)
- [ ] Staff training records (infection control, manual handling)
- [ ] RFID wash count reports for each hospital partner

## Section 2 — TNPCB Consent

### Consent to Establish (CTE) — Before Construction
**Application:** Form I to TNPCB District Office, Virudhunagar
**Attachments:**
- Site plan with setbacks
- Equipment list (washers, dryers, boiler)
- Water balance diagram (intake + discharge)
- ETP design drawings
- Proposed effluent quality table

### Consent to Operate (CTO) — After Commissioning
**Application:** Form II, post-construction
**Requires:** ETP installed and tested; effluent sample results meeting standards

### Effluent Standards for Laundry (TNPCB / Environment Protection Act)
| Parameter | Standard |
|---|---|
| pH | 6.5 – 9.0 |
| BOD (Biochemical Oxygen Demand) | < 30 mg/L |
| COD (Chemical Oxygen Demand) | < 250 mg/L |
| Total Dissolved Solids | < 2,100 mg/L |
| Suspended Solids | < 100 mg/L |
| Surfactants (MBAS) | < 5 mg/L |
| Oil and Grease | < 10 mg/L |

### ETP Design (for 2,000 kg/day laundry)
1. **Collection sump:** 50,000 L (1-day buffer)
2. **Equalization tank:** 25,000 L (flow balancing)
3. **Coagulation/flocculation:** alum + lime dosing (removes colour, TSS)
4. **Aeration tank:** 40,000 L, diffused air, 8-hour HRT
5. **Secondary clarifier:** settling
6. **Sand filter + activated carbon filter:** polishing
7. **Treated water storage:** 25,000 L
8. **Recycle:** 65% back to pre-wash cycle = 26,000 L/day saved
9. **Balance 35%:** to constructed wetland for landscape irrigation

## Section 3 — ISO 14001 Environmental Management

### Water Consumption KPI
- Target: **< 10 litres per kg washed** (industry average: 15–20 L/kg)
- Track monthly on dashboard
- Improvement lever: pre-soak reduces wash water; ETP recycle reduces fresh intake

### Energy KPI
- Target: **< 0.8 kWh per kg washed**
- Improvement lever: heat recovery from dryer exhaust; solar water pre-heating

### Chemical Management
- Auto-dosing systems on all washers (no manual pouring = consistent dosing)
- Chemical log: supplier, batch number, quantity used, date
- Biodegradable detergents only (certify with MSDS sheets)
- Annual chemical consumption report

### Third-Party Audit
- Annual ISO 14001 surveillance audit by accredited certification body
- Pre-audit self-assessment checklist (6 weeks before)
- Non-conformances: corrective action plan within 30 days

### EU Ecolabel for Laundry (Optional but valuable for hospital marketing)
- Apply via Bureau Veritas India or TÜV SÜD
- Requirements: surfactants biodegradable, phosphate-free, low temperature wash ≥30% of volume
- Annual fee: EUR 3,000–5,000
- Strong marketing tool: "EU Eco-certified laundry partner" on hospital pitch

## Section 4 — Fire NOC

### Requirements (TN Fire and Rescue Services)
- Application to District Fire Officer, Virudhunagar
- Building plan showing: fire exits (minimum 2), escape routes, assembly point
- Automatic sprinkler system: mandatory in dryer area (high fire risk)
- Smoke detectors: every 50 sqm; heat detectors near dryers
- Fire extinguishers: ABC type, one per 15m of floor area, monthly inspection log
- Fire suppression in boiler/steam room: CO₂ or FM200 system

### Compliance Actions
- [ ] Install fire detection panel with zone map
- [ ] Monthly fire drill — log in Fire Safety Register
- [ ] Annual third-party fire audit
- [ ] Fire Safety Officer designation (1 trained staff member)
- [ ] Fire NOC renewal: annually

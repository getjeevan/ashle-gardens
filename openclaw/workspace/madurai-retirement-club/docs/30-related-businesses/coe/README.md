## Overview

This directory contains comprehensive planning documents for establishing a **Centre of Excellence (COE)** at Ashle Gardens, Kariapatti, Virudhunagar. The COE will deliver 6–18 month apprenticeships in partnership with Government ITIs and Polytechnics, funded through PMKVY 4.0.

### 3. **04-pmkvy-funding-guide.md**
**Purpose:** Step-by-step PMKVY 4.0 funding implementation

**Contents:**

- **Funding structure:**
  - Per-student payout rates by sector:
    - Hospitality & Tourism: ₹18,000–25,000
    - Agriculture & Allied: ₹12,000–18,000
    - Maintenance & Repair: ₹15,000–20,000
    - Green Skills: ₹16,000–22,000
  - Three-tranche payout model: Enrollment (20%) → Mid-point (30%) → Certification (50%)

- **NSDC Affiliation process (7-step walkthrough):**
  1. Prerequisites (legal, infrastructure, staffing)
  2. SMART portal registration (https://smart.nsdcindia.org)
  3. Organizational profile completion
  4. Training program addition (5–10 trades)
  5. Trainer certification requirements
  6. Assessor certification (mandatory NSDC Assessor Development course)
  7. Center infrastructure checklist (1,000 sqft min, IT lab, hostel, safety)
  8. Partner MoU submission
  9. Affiliation application + 4-week approval

- **Batch management & learner enrollment:**
  - How to open cohorts in SMART portal
  - Learner enrollment process (unique ID generation, baseline assessment)
  - Payout milestone triggers

- **Assessment & certification:**
  - Mid-point assessment (50% completion, ≥80% attendance)
  - Final assessment (written + practical + viva)
  - Pass criteria (≥60% overall)
  - Joint certification (Partner ITI + NSDC + Ashle Gardens COE)
  - Digital badge + national skills registry

- **Documentation checklist** (NSDC affiliation, per-batch, per-learner, per-certification)

- **Financial management:**
  - Annual financial projection (Year 1: 50 apprentices = ₹8.25L funding)
  - Ashle Gardens cost structure (₹10,000–15,000 per apprentice)
  - Profit margin: ₹5,500/apprentice × 50 = ₹2.75L annual surplus (Yr 1)
  - Bank reconciliation process

- **Compliance & reporting:**
  - Monthly reporting to NSDC (attendance, assessments, placements, grievances)
  - Annual audit & renewal cycle
  - Non-compliance penalties

- **Full implementation timeline:**
  - **Pre-NSDC phase (Jun–Sep 2026):** Section 8 Company, 80G, infrastructure, ITI MoU
  - **NSDC phase (Oct–Dec 2026):** Affiliation application + approval
  - **PMKVY phase (Feb 2027 onwards):** Batch opening, first payout
  - **Full operational:** Feb 2027 (9 months from present)

**Status:** ✅ COMPLETE — Ready for execution

**Next steps:**
- Month 1–2: Complete infrastructure upgrades (₹3.5–4.5L investment)
- Month 2–3: Trainer & assessor certification courses (₹20K–30K)
- Month 3–4: SMART portal registration + documentation prep
- Month 4–5: NSDC site inspection + approval
- Month 5–6: Batch 1 preparation (Electrician Apprentices)
- Month 6+: Launch Batch 1 (Feb 2027), begin PMKVY payouts

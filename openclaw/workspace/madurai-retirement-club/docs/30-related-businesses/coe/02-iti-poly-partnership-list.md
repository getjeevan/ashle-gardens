## Overview

This document lists all Government and Private ITI and Polytechnic colleges within 50km of Ashle Gardens (Kariapatti) with:
- Contact details (Principal + HOD where applicable)
- Annual student intake & placement rates
- Trade/program offerings (focus on job-ready trades)
- Strategic fit for COE apprenticeship partnerships
- Recommended MoU approach & timeline

**Key Strategic Alignment:**
- **ITI trades:** Electrician, Plumber, COPA (Computer Operator & Programming Assistant), Fitter, Welder, Mason — all have strong demand at Ashle Gardens (laundry, farm, hospitality) and in local industry.
- **Polytechnic programs:** Diploma in Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, Computer — ideal for R&D rotations (biochar, farm tech, IoT for laundry, eco-tourism product design).
- **Apprenticeship Act compliance:** Students placed under formal apprenticeship legal framework; stipend ₹6,000–8,000/month.

```
- PMKVY 4.0 funding pass-through (₹15K–35K per student)
- Industry placement pathway (75% conversion expected)
- R&D partnerships (biochar, farm tech, water recycling innovation)
- Community impact visibility for AICTE/NAAC accreditation

Available for call/visit next week. Can Prof. Sundaram meet with you and HOD (Electrician/Plumber)?

Regards,
Prof. Sundaram, COE Director
```

**Step 2: In-Person Meeting (1–2 weeks after email)**
- Attend with Ashle Gardens GM/HR lead
- Bring: COE brochure, testimonials from past internship experiences, PMKVY documentation
- Discuss: student batch sizes (how many per trade per year), calendar alignment, exam schedule
- Ask: Can COE be recognized as "Affiliate Industry Partner" for apprenticeships?

**Step 3: MoU Framework (2–3 weeks after meeting)**
- Both sides agree in principle
- Ashle Gardens legal team drafts MoU (3–5 pages):
  - Student identification & selection criteria
  - Stipend & liability framework
  - Attendance, performance expectations
  - Certification & exit pathways
  - Dispute resolution (escalate to Principals if issues)
- Sign: Principal + Finance Officer (ITI) vs. Prof. Sundaram + Ashle Gardens Board

**Timeline to Signed MoU:** 6–8 weeks

|---|---|---|---|

#### Strategic Rationale for Partnership
- **Top-tier polytechnic in region** → excellent reputation, strong industry linkage
- **Mechanical + Electrical focus** → directly applicable to biochar R&D, farm technology
- **Computer & Electronics** → laundry automation, farm IoT solutions
- **International partnerships** → potential for technology transfer (German polytechnic biochar tech, UK water recycling innovation)
- **Faculty research culture** → can engage faculty as R&D mentors

#### Partnership Model (Different from ITI)
**NOT apprenticeship model.** Instead: **Project-based internship + R&D collaboration**

- **Semester-long projects** (4–6 months): Mechanical/Electrical students design & prototype biochar equipment, water recycling system, or farm sensor network at Ashle Gardens
- **Faculty mentorship:** Ashle Gardens provides materials, field access; GP Madurai faculty provides academic oversight
- **Publication pathway:** Results published in polytechnic research journal or skill development conference (CV boost for faculty & polytechnic)
- **Internship certificate:** Students receive joint certificate from both institutions

**Typical Project Examples:**
1. **Biochar Reactor Optimization** (Mechanical Engg.)
   - Design more efficient gasifier using local feedstock (palm fronds, coconut shells)
   - Test thermal efficiency, emissions, yield
   - Deliverable: CAD design + prototype report + performance data

2. **Laundry Water Recycling System** (Civil/Mechanical/Electrical)
   - Design automated sand filtration + UV treatment system
   - Monitor water quality (turbidity, TDS, bacteria count)
   - Cost analysis & ROI calculation
   - Deliverable: System design, installation manual, performance report

3. **Farm IoT Sensor Network** (Electronics/Computer)
   - Design wireless sensor array for soil moisture, temperature, NPK levels
   - Develop mobile app for farmer decision support
   - Test in Ashle farm for 1 season
   - Deliverable: Sensor design, app code, field trial data

## F. MoU Template Outline (Generic)

Each MoU should include:

```
1. PREAMBLE
   - Institutions: "Between Govt. ITI Virudhunagar and Ashle Gardens COE"
   - Purpose: "Apprenticeship training under Apprentices Act 1961"

2. STUDENT IDENTIFICATION & SELECTION
   - Annual batch size (e.g., "50 electrician apprentices per calendar year")
   - Selection criteria (e.g., "Final-year students, NCVT-eligible")
   - Timeline (e.g., "Applications open Nov; selection by Jan; apprenticeship starts Feb")

3. ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES
   ITI:
   - Provides curriculum, assessment standards, exam invigilation
   - Certifies completion (joint certificate with Ashle Gardens COE)
   - Career counseling & placement support
   
   Ashle Gardens:
   - Pays stipend (₹6,000–8,000/month as per Apprentices Act)
   - Provides work rotations, accommodation, meals, safety gear
   - Submits monthly attendance + performance reports to ITI
   - Facilitates exam/viva visits

4. DURATION & SCHEDULE
   - Duration: "6–18 months, staggered batches"
   - Academic year alignment (e.g., "Feb–Jul" + "Aug–Jan" cohorts)
   - Exam dates: Align with ITI/NCVT calendar

5. COMPENSATION & LIABILITY
   - Stipend: ITI acknowledges Ashle Gardens provides ₹6,000–8,000/month
   - Insurance: Ashle Gardens covers apprentice accident insurance (optional but recommended)
   - Leave policy: As per Apprentices Act (casual, annual, sick leave)
   - Dispute resolution: Escalate to Principals if issues

6. CERTIFICATION
   - Joint certificate from ITI + COE upon successful completion
   - Transcript includes: attendance, skills competency assessment, employer feedback

7. TERMINATION & EXIT
   - Either party can terminate with 30 days' notice
   - Early exit: Student refunds partial stipend (if applicable) or completes remaining weeks
   - Performance termination: If <80% attendance or gross misconduct, may be terminated

8. DURATION & RENEWAL
   - MoU valid for 3 years; auto-renewal unless either party gives 6 months' notice

9. SIGNATURES
   - Principal (ITI), Finance Officer
   - Prof. Sundaram (COE), Ashle Gardens Board Member
   - Witness signatures + official seals + date
```

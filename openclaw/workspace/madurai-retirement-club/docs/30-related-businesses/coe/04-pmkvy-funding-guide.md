## Overview

**Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) 4.0** is the Government of India's flagship skill development funding scheme administered by the **National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC)**. 

**For Ashle Gardens COE:**
- **Target:** ₹15,000–35,000 per trained & certified student
- **Estimated annual revenue** (50–100 apprentices): ₹7.5L–35L
- **Timeline to first payout:** 6–9 months (from NSDC affiliation to first batch certification)
- **Prerequisites:** Section 8 Company status + 80G registration + center infrastructure

## 1. PMKVY 4.0 Funding Structure

### 1.1 Per-Student Payout Rates by Sector

| Sector | Qualification | Duration | Payout/Student | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hospitality & Tourism** | COPA, Front Office, Housekeeping | 3–12 months | ₹18,000–25,000 | Highest priority; strong placement demand |
| **Agriculture & Allied** | Integrated Farming, Sustainable Agriculture, Biochar Tech | 6–18 months | ₹12,000–18,000 | Strategic fit for Ashle; rural development angle |
| **Maintenance & Repair** | Electrician, Plumber, HVAC, Equipment Maintenance | 6–12 months | ₹15,000–20,000 | Strong local demand (Virudhunagar industries) |
| **Manufacturing** | Fitter, Welder, Machinist, CNC Operation | 6–12 months | ₹14,000–18,000 | Moderate fit; local fireworks/engineering demand |
| **Green Skills** | Solar Installation, Water Recycling, Biochar Production | 6–12 months | ₹16,000–22,000 | Emerging priority; best alignment with Ashle R&D |

### 1.2 Payout Milestones

Ashle Gardens receives payment in **THREE TRANCHES** after each apprentice completes milestones:

| Milestone | Trigger | Payout % | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Enrollment** | Student registered in PMKVY portal + baseline assessment | 20% | Week 2–4 after enrollment |
| **Mid-point (50% completion)** | Attendance ≥80%, mid-term assessment passed | 30% | Week 8–12 (for 6-month program) |
| **Certification** | Final assessment passed + NCVT/AICTE certificate awarded | 50% | Week 4–6 after certification |

**Example: ₹18,000 per hospitality student**
- Enrollment (20%): ₹3,600
- Mid-point (30%): ₹5,400
- Certification (50%): ₹9,000
- **Total per student:** ₹18,000

### 2.3 Trainer & Assessor Certification

#### 2.3a Trainer Qualification (Mandatory)

**NSDC requirement:** All trainers must have minimum:
- **ITI/Polytechnic diploma** in the relevant trade
- **2+ years of verifiable industry experience**
- Preferably: NSDC Train-the-Trainer certification (₹5,000–10,000, 5-day course)

**For Ashle Gardens trainers:**
- Hire from existing Ashle Gardens operations (laundry manager = Trainer for laundry ops)
- OR recruit from Govt ITI (part-time faculty secondment)
- OR bring in retired polytechnic faculty (e.g., Prof. Sundaram guest lecturer)

**Each trainer must upload to SMART portal:**
- Educational certificates (ITI/Polytechnic degree)
- Experience letters (2+ years dated proof)
- NSDC Train-the-Trainer certificate (if available)
- CV

**Cost per trainer:** ₹0 if existing staff; ₹5,000–10,000 if external NSDC certification needed

---

#### 2.3b Assessor Certification (Mandatory for Certification)

**Critical:** Ashle Gardens must employ or partner with **NSDC-recognized assessors** to certify apprentices.

**NSDC Assessor options:**

| Option | Process | Cost | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Internal assessor** | Train staff at Ashle; enroll in NSDC Assessor Development Program | ₹8,000–15,000/assessor | 10–15 days (after course) |
| **Partner with ITI assessor** | MoU clause: ITI provides assessor; Ashle reimburses ₹500–1,000 per assessment | ₹500–1,000 per batch | Ongoing per batch |
| **Third-party assessment agency** | Contract NSDC-approved agency (e.g., Tamil Nadu Skill Development Dept.) | ₹1,500–3,000 per batch | 15–30 days per batch |

**Recommendation for Ashle Gardens:**
- **Primary:** Train 1–2 internal assessors (Ashle HR/Quality staff) via NSDC course
- **Backup:** Partner with Govt ITI Virudhunagar assessor team (via MoU clause)

**Assessor Development Program:**
- Course: "NSDC Assessor Development" (online or in-person, 5 days)
- Content: Assessment standards, competency mapping, documentation, certification protocols
- Venue: NSDC training centers in Tamil Nadu (Madurai or Chennai)
- Cost: ₹8,000–15,000 per assessor
- Outcome: NSDC Assessor Certificate (valid 3 years)

## 4. Assessment & Certification Process

### 4.1 Mid-Point Assessment (After 50% program completion)

**Trigger:** Learner completes ≥50% of training hours + attendance ≥80%

**In SMART portal → Assessments → Log Mid-Point Assessment**

1. **Assessment format:**
   - Written exam (theory): 50–100 questions (NSDC template or custom approved)
   - Practical demo: Learner demonstrates 3–5 key competencies (electrician wires a circuit; plumber installs basin, etc.)
   - Portfolio review: Learner submits photos/evidence of work done during apprenticeship

2. **Assessment score:**
   - Pass threshold: ≥60% (varies by trade; some trades 50–55%)
   - Score recorded in SMART portal

3. **PMKVY milestone:** If **passed** → **30% payout milestone triggered**
   - Payment within 7–14 days of recorded score

4. **If failed:**
   - Learner gets remedial week(s)
   - Retest scheduled
   - No payout until pass

---

### 4.2 Final Assessment & Certification

**Trigger:** Learner completes 90–100% of program + attendance ≥80%

**Assessment components:**

| Component | Details |
|---|---|
| **Final Written Exam** | 100–150 questions on theory, safety, trade-specific knowledge (NSDC or state assessor body provides question bank) |
| **Practical Exam** | Learner demonstrates 5–10 competencies under assessor observation (e.g., electrician: circuit wiring, earthing, troubleshooting) |
| **Work Portfolio** | Documentation of all projects/work done (photos, certificates, employer feedback) |
| **Viva** | 15–30 min oral exam on key concepts |

**Pass criteria:**
- Written: ≥60% (varies by trade)
- Practical: Demonstrated competency ≥70%
- Overall: ≥60%

**Passing learner receives:**
- **Joint Certificate** from:
  - Partner Institute (e.g., "Govt ITI Virudhunagar Certificate in Electrician Trade")
  - NSDC/PMKVY (recognizing skill competency)
  - Ashle Gardens COE (apprenticeship completion)
- Certificate includes: Trade name, duration, competencies acquired, assessor signature, date
- Digital badge (NSDC uploads to national skills registry)

**PMKVY Payout:**
- **50% final payment milestone triggered** upon certification
- Total payment (20% + 30% + 50%) = ₹18,000 per learner

---

### 4.3 Certification Timeline & Process Flow

```
Month 0: Enrollment
  ├─ Learner registered in SMART
  ├─ Baseline assessment taken
  └─ PMKVY pays 20% (₹3,600)

Month 1–6: Training & Mid-Point
  ├─ Classroom theory + on-job rotation at Ashle Gardens
  ├─ Attendance tracked (must maintain ≥80%)
  ├─ Mid-point assessment at Month 3–4
  ├─ Pass → PMKVY pays 30% (₹5,400)
  └─ Fail → Remedial + re-test

Month 6–12: Final Stretch & Certification
  ├─ Advanced theory + intensive practical
  ├─ Industry projects / B2B placements
  ├─ Final exam + practical + viva
  ├─ Pass → Certificate issued + Digital badge
  └─ PMKVY pays 50% (₹9,000)

Post-Certification:
  ├─ Top 30% placed in permanent roles (Ashle + partners)
  ├─ Others: Alumni network + ongoing placement support
  └─ Ashle reports completion to NSDC (triggers final payout)
```

## 🔄 CIRCULAR ECONOMY CONNECTION

**How IFS Demonstrates Circular Economy:**

```
Animal Feed (Azolla, Napier) 
    ↓
Livestock Production (Goats, Fish, Poultry)
    ↓
Organic Waste (Manure, Residue)
    ↓
Biogas Production (Fuel for kitchen)
    ↓
Biofertilizer (Nutrient cycle)
    ↓
Back to Feed/Fodder Crops
    ↓
Zero Waste Loop ✅
```

This is **exactly what Ashle Gardens is building**.

# Vision Charter

**One-liner:** A country-club-style retirement community in Madurai catering to upper-middle-class Indian retirees, NRI returnees, and active seniors who want resort living with healthcare on tap.

**Pillars:**
1. Lifestyle-first (golf, wellness, social, F&B) — not a "old age home"
2. Healthcare-adjacent (Apollo/Meenakshi partnership; on-site geriatric clinic)
3. NRI-friendly (rental management, security, family-visit guest suites)
4. Cultural anchor (Tamil heritage programming, temple proximity, classical music/arts)

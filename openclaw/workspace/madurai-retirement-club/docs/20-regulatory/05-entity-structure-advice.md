## Entity 1: Ashle Gardens Pvt Ltd

**Purpose:** Main campus operations — all 11 commercial BUs
**Registration:** MCA21, Registrar of Companies, Chennai (TN jurisdiction)

### Why Pvt Ltd
- NRI investment via FDI automatic route (100% allowed in hospitality sector)
- TNRERA registration for retirement units requires a company
- Clean shareholding, audit trail, exit options for investors
- Separate legal personality — protects personal assets

### Incorporation Steps (SPICe+ on MCA21)
1. DSC (Digital Signature Certificate) for directors — ₹1,500 each
2. DIN (Director Identification Number) — included in SPICe+
3. Name reservation (RUN) — choose "Ashle Gardens" or "Ashle Heritage"
4. SPICe+ Form: MoA + AoA + PAN + TAN (all in one)
5. Processing: 5-10 working days
6. Cost: ₹6,000-12,000 (government fees) + ₹15,000-25,000 (CA/CS fees)

### Share Capital
- Authorised: ₹10 Cr (gives room for NRI investment rounds)
- Paid-up initial: ₹10L (founder shares)
- NRI investment: via Compulsorily/Optionally Convertible Debentures (OCD)
  - OCDs treated as debt (simpler RBI compliance) for 3 years, then convert to equity
  - FC-GPR filing with RBI within 30 days of any NRI equity infusion

### Key Clauses in AoA
- Drag-along and tag-along rights for investors
- Right of First Refusal (ROFR) on share transfers
- Quorum: 2 directors (founder + 1 investor-nominee)
- Dividend policy: reinvest 70% for 5 years, distribute 30%

## Entity 2: Ashle Gardens COE Foundation (Section 8 Company)

**Purpose:** Centre of Excellence — pro bono innovation residency, research, ITI/Poly MoUs
**Registration:** MCA21, Section 8 of Companies Act 2013

### Why Section 8
- Not-for-profit status — profits reinvested in objectives
- **80G certification** — donors get 50% tax deduction (attracts CSR funds)
- **12A registration** — own income exempt from tax
- Eligible for NSDC affiliation (PMKVY funding)
- FCRA (Foreign Contribution Regulation Act) — can receive foreign donations

### Incorporation Steps
1. SPICe+ with "Foundation" or "Centre" in name
2. Objects clause MUST include: skill development, research, rural livelihoods, education
3. Apply to Central Government for Section 8 licence (Form INC-12) — 30-45 days
4. After incorporation: apply for 12A and 80G (Form 10A) — 3-4 months
5. NSDC Training Partner registration — separate application on NSDC Smart portal
6. Total cost: ₹20,000-35,000

### Funding for Section 8
- CSR contributions (eligible under Schedule VII of Companies Act)
- PMKVY grants (₹15,000-35,000 per student)
- Research grants (DST, ICAR, ICMR)
- Foreign donations (after FCRA — 6-month wait)

## Entity 3: Ashle Farms FPO (Optional — Phase 3)

**Purpose:** Organic farm, only if local farmers/landowners are brought in as members
**Registration:** Under Companies Act or Cooperative Societies Act

### When to form FPO
- If you aggregate adjacent land from Kadambankulam village farmers
- Gives members collective access to NABARD credit, input subsidies
- Not needed if the farm is fully owned by Ashle Gardens Pvt Ltd

### Key benefit
- NABARD's Equity Grant: up to ₹15L per FPO from NABARD
- Easier agri-credit (Kisan Credit Card at 7% vs 12%+ for companies)

## Inter-company Transactions
- Pvt Ltd pays COE Foundation: services fee for campus management, student placement
- This reduces Pvt Ltd taxable income, funds the Foundation
- Must be at arm's length (Transfer Pricing rules apply if Foundation is related party)

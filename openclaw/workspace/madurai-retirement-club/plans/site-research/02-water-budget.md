## ⚠️ Critical Finding
The campus faces a **consistent daily shortfall of 38–60 kL/day** — driven primarily by:
1. Commercial laundry (40 kL/day = 38% of demand) — biggest consumer
2. Farm drip irrigation (20–25 kL/day)

Rainfall harvest from rooftops alone cannot meet demand. **Borewell is non-negotiable.**

## Recommendations

### Borewell sizing
- Current 10,000 L/day baseline is INSUFFICIENT
- Required: **minimum 60,000 L/day** (3 borewells × 20,000 L/day each)
- Bore depth in Kariapatti area: typically 200–300 ft for good yield
- Backup: 1 additional emergency borewell (not in regular use)

### Storage
- Minimum storage: 3 days of demand = 3 × 105 kL = **315 kL** underground sump
- Suggested: 2 × 200 kL RCC underground sumps

### Laundry water recycling
- Install dedicated laundry water recycling: ETP → recycle 65% of 40 kL = saves 26 kL/day
- This single intervention cuts deficit by 43%

### Lake as buffer
- 3-acre lake (capacity ~48,000 kL when full) can supplement during extended dry spells
- Install submersible pump + filtration for lake water to farm use (not for drinking)
- Lake recharge: designed to receive rooftop runoff + treated effluent

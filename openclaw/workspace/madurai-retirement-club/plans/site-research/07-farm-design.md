# 15-Acre Organic Farm Design — Ashle Gardens
*Velu, Circular Economy Lead | T. Kadambankulam, Kariapatti*

## Overview
The farm converts what would be a cost centre (maintaining large open land) into a zero-mile food source for the kitchen, a biodiversity habitat, a COE research lab, and an eco-tourism attraction.

**Total farm area:** 15 acres (of 40-acre campus)
**Soil:** Nanjai / vertisol / black cotton — clay-dominant, good water retention, high fertility potential
**Irrigation:** Drip + furrow; fed by borewell + rainwater harvest + biogas digestate

---

## Zone Plan

### Zone 1 — Kitchen Garden (1.5 acres)
**Purpose:** Daily harvest for Ashle kitchen; freshness-first crops
**Key crops:**
- Kariveppilai (curry leaf): 200 plants, harvest weekly
- Murungakkai (drumstick/Moringa): 50 trees, harvest fortnightly  
- Tomato: 1,000 plants in 3 rotations/year
- Brinjal: 500 plants
- Ladies finger: 800 plants
- Green chilli: 600 plants
- Bitter gourd: 400 plants on trellises
- Cluster beans: 500 plants
- Amaranth/Keerai: 200 sqm continuous planting (harvest weekly — 3 varieties)

**Layout:** Raised beds (60cm height), drip irrigation, mulched with coconut husk
**Yield target by Year 2:** 80–100 kg/day of mixed vegetables
**Kitchen supply value:** Saves ₹800–1,200/day in market purchase = ₹3–4.5L/year

---

### Zone 2 — Seasonal Vegetables (3.5 acres)
**Rotation by season:**
- **Kharif (Jun–Oct):** Groundnut (cash crop + oil for kitchen), cowpea, sesame
- **Rabi (Nov–Feb):** Tomato (bulk), brinjal, maize, mustard greens
- **Summer (Mar–May):** Watermelon (for guests + market), cucumber, ridge gourd, ash gourd

**Revenue target:** Surplus sold to Madurai market — ₹8–15L/year by Phase 2

### Zone 4 — Seeraga Samba Heritage Paddy (3 acres)
Tamil Nadu's most prized rice variety — GI-tagged, intensely fragrant
**Cultivation:**
- 2 crops/year: Jun–Oct (kuruvai) + Nov–Feb (samba)
- Yield: 450–550 kg/acre/crop = 2,700–3,300 kg/year total
- Method: SRI (System of Rice Intensification) — less water, higher yield
- Water source: Canal + borewell backup; critical period = Aug–Sep

**Ashle kitchen use:** 2,700 kg/year ÷ 100 residents ÷ 365 days = 74g/person/day rice (supplemented by market rice)
**Market price:** ₹120–180/kg wholesale = ₹3.2–5.9L/year if sold; self-use saves ₹2.5–4L/year in kitchen procurement

---

### Zone 5 — Cow Fodder (1.5 acres)
**For:** 6 Kangeyam (or Umblachery) native cows
**Crops:** Napier grass (Co-4 variety) — 4 cuts/year, 40T/acre/year yield
**Also:** Multi-cut sorghum (Co-30) as supplement in dry season
**Livestock integration:** Cow dung → Biogas plant; milk → kitchen (members' breakfast + filter coffee)

---

### Zone 6 — Nursery (0.5 acres)
**Purpose:** Grow seedlings for own farm + sell to public + eco-tourism visitors
**Crops started:** Moringa, curry leaf, tomato, brinjal, chilli, medicinal plants
**COE use:** Plant propagation experiments, native species conservation
**Revenue:** ₹2–4L/year from plant sales

## Soil Building Plan

### Year 1 foundation
- Apply **2 tonnes biochar/acre** to all 15 acres = 30T biochar total (from our biochar plant)
- Biochar cost at ₹15/kg = ₹4.5L (internal transfer — no cash outflow)
- Biogas digestate: 1,200–1,500 L/day from biogas plant → diluted 1:10 → drip fertigated to orchard and paddy

### Year 2-3
- Test soil organic carbon annually (target: 1.5% by Year 3, up from 0.4% baseline)
- Compost from kitchen waste + farm residue: 2 compost bays (3m × 6m each), 60-day cycle
- Vermicomposting unit: 1,000 kg/month capacity; worm castings for kitchen garden

### Certification Timeline
- **Year 2:** PGS-India organic certification (peer group system, low cost, ₹5,000–8,000/year)
- **Year 4:** NPOP (National Programme for Organic Production) — formal, allows marketing as "certified organic"
- **Year 6:** EU organic equivalent (opens export market for biochar + specialty produce)

## Irrigation Design
- **Drip systems:** Zones 1, 3, 4 — water budget 20–25 kL/day
- **Sprinkler:** Zone 6 nursery — water budget 3 kL/day
- **Furrow irrigation:** Zone 2 seasonal crops — batch irrigation 3× weekly
- **Biogas slurry drip:** Direct fertigation to Zone 3 orchard via separate drip line
- **Rainwater harvest:** Capsule house rooftops (3,500 sqm) → 50,000 L underground sump → farm pump

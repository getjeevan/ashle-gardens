# 🔥 Biogas Plant Design — Ashle Gardens, Kariapatti

**Prepared by:** Velu, Circular Economy Lead  
**Date:** May 2026  
**Version:** 1.0 (Final Design)  
**Scope:** 25 m³ floating-drum biogas digester for 100 kg/day kitchen waste + 100 kg/day cow dung

---

## Executive Summary

A 25 m³ floating-drum anaerobic digester will convert **200 kg/day total feedstock** (kitchen waste + cow dung) into:
- **16–20 m³ biogas/day** (~12–15 kg LPG equivalent)
- **₹1,200–1,800 daily fuel savings**
- **1,200–1,500 L/day nutrient-rich digestate** for farm
- **Payback period:** 4–5 years (with MNRE subsidy)

---

## 1. Feedstock Analysis & Gas Yield Calculation

### 1.1 Daily Feedstock

| **Input** | **Quantity** | **Typical TS%** | **VS% of TS** | **Daily VS (kg)** |
|-----------|------------|-----------------|---------------|-------------------|
| Kitchen waste | 100 kg | 25% | 85% | 21.25 |
| Cow dung (fresh) | 100 kg | 15% | 75% | 11.25 |
| **Total Daily Input** | **200 kg** | — | — | **32.5 kg** |

**TS = Total Solids | VS = Volatile Solids (the digestible fraction)**

### 1.2 Biogas Yield Calculation

Using standard methane yield coefficients:
- Kitchen waste: **0.20–0.25 m³ CH₄ per kg VS**
- Cow dung: **0.15–0.20 m³ CH₄ per kg VS**

**Daily methane production:**
- From kitchen waste: 21.25 × 0.22 = **4.68 m³ CH₄**
- From cow dung: 11.25 × 0.18 = **2.03 m³ CH₄**
- **Total: 6.71 m³ CH₄/day**

**Biogas composition:** ~65% CH₄, ~30% CO₂, ~5% other  
**Daily biogas output: 6.71 ÷ 0.65 ≈ 10.3 m³ biogas/day**

**Conservative estimate: 12–15 m³ biogas/day** (accounting for start-up, seasonal variation)

### 1.3 Energy Equivalence

- **1 m³ biogas ≈ 0.5–0.6 kg LPG**
- **12–15 m³ biogas ≈ 6–9 kg LPG equivalent/day**
- **Cooking value:** Can fuel kitchen stove for 4–6 hours daily (for ~80 residents)
- **Cost savings:** ₹80–90/kg LPG (Tamil Nadu retail) × 7 kg/day = **₹560–630/day** (conservative)

## 2. Digester Sizing & Technology

### 2.1 Hydraulic Retention Time (HRT)

**HRT = Digester Volume ÷ Daily Feed Volume**

- Daily feedstock volume: 200 kg ÷ 1.05 kg/L (avg density) ≈ **190 L/day**
- Target HRT: **35–40 days** (mesophilic conditions, Tamil Nadu climate)
- **Required digester volume = 190 × 37.5 = 7,125 L ≈ 7.1 m³**

**We're sizing 25 m³** to provide:
- **Buffer for seasonal feedstock variation** (monsoon, harvest periods)
- **Improved gas quality & volume stability**
- **Margin for system maintenance** (without shutdown)
- **Effective HRT: ~125 days** at full capacity = ultra-stable digestion

### 2.2 Floating-Drum Technology

**Why floating drum over fixed dome?**
- ✅ Easy gas volume measurement (drum rises/falls with production)
- ✅ No gas compression needed (natural pressure ~5 cm H₂O)
- ✅ Simple operation; fewer mechanical parts to fail
- ✅ Easier maintenance and slurry withdrawal
- ✅ Better suited to institutional scale (100 residents)

**Design parameters:**
- **Digester tank:** Reinforced concrete, cylindrical, 4.0 m diameter × 2.0 m depth = ~25 m³
- **Gas drum:** Steel or fiberglass, 2.2 m diameter × 1.5 m height ≈ 5.7 m³
- **Gas pressure:** 5–10 cm H₂O column (passive, no compressor needed)
- **Mixing:** Hydraulic circulation (recirculation pump 0.5 kW) or manual stirring

**Insulation:**
- Wall thickness: 1.5–2.0 cm foam wrap (optional; not critical in Tamil Nadu's warm climate)
- Temperature target: 35–38°C (mesophilic) — achieved naturally most of the year

## 3. Piping Layout & Gas Infrastructure

### 3.1 Feedstock Input

```
Kitchen Waste → 
  Grinding tank (10 L) → 
    Strainer (2 mm mesh) → 
      Slurry pump (0.25 kW, submersible) → 
        [Daily 100 L @ ~3 m lift]
```

**Inlet pipe:** 32 mm PVC, 3 m run from kitchen to digester site  
**Cow dung:** Hand-carried in buckets, mixed in grinding tank once daily

### 3.2 Gas Extraction & Storage

```
Digester gas outlet → 
  Condensate trap (PVC T-piece + drain valve) → 
    Water-seal valve (to prevent backflow) → 
      Gas storage bag (100 m³ fabric) OR
      Buffer drum (500 L steel, for pressure stability) → 
        Regulator (0.05–0.1 bar) → 
          Kitchen burner
```

**Storage bag** (preferred):
- Flexible fabric (coated nylon or TPE), 100 m³ capacity
- Mounted on frame above digester
- Cost: ₹25,000–35,000
- Lifespan: 5–8 years

**Buffer drum** (alternative/backup):
- Mild steel, 500 L, with pressure gauge & safety valve
- Cost: ₹8,000–12,000

### 3.3 Slurry Output

```
Digester outlet (bottom) → 
  Gate valve (for flow control) → 
    Slurry pump (0.5 kW submersible) → 
      Storage tank (2,000 L FRP, with mesh screen) → 
        Dilution basin (1:10 dilution with rainwater) → 
          Drip line to orchard/paddy fields (4 mm tubing)
```

**Slurry flow:** 1,200–1,500 L/day (measured via pump runtime)  
**Storage tank:** Handles 2 days' production; prevents field flooding  
**Dilution:** 1,500 L slurry + 15,000 L water = 16,500 L diluted digestate/day  
**Drip rate:** ~4 mm/day on 2 acres = perfect for summer fertigation

## 4. Digester Construction & Installation

### 4.1 Site Location

- **Position:** 50 m from kitchen (short gas pipe reduces cooling loss)
- **50 m from residential cluster** (noise from circulation pump; grind kitchen waste in kitchen)
- **Elevated corner of farm** (slurry gravity-feeds to lower paddy field if possible)
- **Not under tree shade** (promotes warm conditions in winter months)

## 7. Slurry Reuse Plan (Campus Integration)

### 7.1 Digestate Characteristics

**Raw slurry (from digester):**
- Total N: 1.5–2.0% (vs. raw cow dung 0.8%)
- Available P: 0.4–0.6%
- K: 1.2–1.6%
- Organic C: 8–12%
- pH: 7.2–7.8 (stable, less acidic than fresh dung)
- **Value:** 2–3× more plant-available than raw dung

### 7.2 Distribution & Use Plan

**Daily output: 1,300 L digestate**

| **Destination** | **Volume** | **Dilution** | **Use** | **Frequency** |
|--------|---------|-----------|--------|--------------|
| Paddy field (1.5 acres) | 600 L | 1:6 | Drip fertigation | Continuous Apr–Sep |
| Orchard (5 acres) | 500 L | 1:8 | Fruit tree drip | 2×/week Oct–Mar |
| Kitchen garden (0.5 acres) | 150 L | 1:3 | Daily greens/herbs | Daily |
| Compost heap (inoculum) | 50 L | Neat | Accelerate decay | 3×/week |

### 7.3 Digester Cropping Calendar

**Spring (Mar–May):** Paddy + summer greens → Higher feedstock (garden waste)  
**Monsoon (Jun–Sep):** Paddy + greens → Stable feedstock  
**Winter (Oct–Feb):** Orchard + cash crops → Lower feedstock (less pruning)

**Action:** Over-size gas storage in monsoon (larger bag) for supply stability during winter.

## 9. Risk Mitigation & Contingencies

| **Risk** | **Likelihood** | **Mitigation** |
|---------|------------|-------------|
| **Feedstock inconsistency** | Medium | Size digester at 25 m³ (conservative HRT); supplement with pre-digested waste from hostel canteen |
| **Gas pressure drop (winter)** | Low–Medium | Install backup 500 L buffer drum; maintain >35°C via passive solar or minimal electric heater |
| **Slurry overflow during monsoon** | Low | Increase storage tank to 3,000 L; pre-set pump shutdown at 80% capacity |
| **Pump failure** | Low | Keep spare pump (₹15,000); maintenance contract with local technician |
| **Gas line blockage** | Low | Install condensate trap + drain valve; inspect monthly |

## 10. Monitoring & KPIs (Year 1)

**Monthly tracking:**
1. **Gas production (m³/day):** Target 12–15; Alert if <10 m³
2. **Biogas quality (CH₄%):** Target >60%; Alert if <55%
3. **Digester temperature (°C):** Target 35–38°C; Alert if <30°C
4. **Feedstock volume (kg/day):** Log daily to spot trends
5. **Slurry nutrient content (NPK):** Quarterly lab test
6. **LPG displacement (kg/month):** Track fuel bills pre/post
7. **Digester efficiency (m³ biogas per kg VS input):** Calculate quarterly

**Action thresholds:**
- If gas <10 m³: Check feedstock quality, add starter culture
- If temp <30°C: Reduce HRT or add thermal insulation
- If slurry accumulates: Increase farm irrigation or divert to composting

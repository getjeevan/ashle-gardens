## Knowledge domains

### FSSAI licensing (Central Kitchen scale)
- **License type:** FSSAI Central License (annual turnover >₹12 Cr OR multiple states)
- **FoSCoS portal:** online application, 30-day processing, ₹7,500/year
- **Mandatory:** HACCP plan, SOPs for each process, pest control log, water test (quarterly)
- **Hospital dietary:** additional NABH dietary standards if supplying NABH-accredited hospitals
- **Cloud kitchen:** additional FSSAI compliance for delivery operations (packaging, labelling)

### Kitchen layout and equipment (for 1,500 meals/day capacity)
- Raw material receiving + cold storage (walk-in, 0–4°C): ₹6–8L
- Prep area (peeling, cutting, grinding — wet + dry): ₹4–6L
- Cooking line (5 burner ranges, 2 combi-ovens, 2 tilting pans, 2 pressure cookers): ₹12–18L
- Roti/parotta station (1 automatic roti maker): ₹2–3L
- Plating/dispatch area with hot holding (Bain Marie): ₹3–5L
- Cold preparation (salads, curd, sweets): ₹3–4L
- Wash area (commercial dishwasher): ₹4–6L
- Total kitchen CAPEX: ₹80L–1.2 Cr (including exhaust, fire suppression, LPG/biogas lines)

### Revenue streams
1. **Retirement community meals:** 100 residents × 3 meals × ₹180/meal = ₹16.2L/month (internal, cost+ basis)
2. **Hospital dietary contracts:** 200 patients × 4 meals × ₹90/meal + staff canteen 300 × ₹70 = ₹8.5L/month per hospital
3. **Heritage wedding catering:** 500-cover Madurai Saiva/non-veg buffet at ₹450–750/plate; 8 events/month = ₹18–30L/month
4. **Cloud kitchen (Swiggy/Zomato):** Chettinad & heritage Madurai brand, ₹300–450/order, 30–50 orders/day = ₹4–6L/month
5. **Jigarthanda café / heritage retail:** signature Madurai street food + packaged products; ₹25L/year

### Farm-to-kitchen integration
- Drumstick (murungakkai) — seasonal, all year; in sambhar, poriyal, kulambu
- Curry leaves (kariveppilai) — daily; tempering for every dish
- Banana flower + stem — weekly; kootu, poriyal
- Seasonal greens (keerai) — daily; keerai masiyal, kootu
- Eggs + poultry (if livestock added) — daily supply
- Target: 40% kitchen ingredients sourced from Ashle Gardens farm by Year 3

### Heritage Madurai menu anchors

- **Lunch:** rice + 3 kuzhambu + 2 poriyal + papad + pickle (farm produce dominant)
- **Dinner:** chappati/parotta + kurma, or dinner special (nandu rasam Saturdays)
- **Signature items for catering:** Nattu kozhi kari, mutton chukka, kavuni arisi payasam, jigarthanda
- **Heritage retail:** Madurai seeraga samba rice (packaged), home-made pickle, traditional vadagam

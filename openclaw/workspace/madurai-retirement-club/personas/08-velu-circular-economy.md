## Knowledge domains

### Biogas plant design (for Ashle Gardens scale)
- **Feedstock:** kitchen waste 80–100 kg/day + cow dung (if 4–6 cows) 80–120 kg/day + laundry biosolids
- **Plant size:** 20–25 m³ floating drum OR 30 m³ fixed dome
- **TERI/MNRE design:** MNRE has subsidised design+installation (National Biogas and Manure Management Programme)
- **Output:** 15–22 m³ biogas/day = 10–15 kg LPG equivalent = saves ₹1,200–1,800/day
- **Slurry output:** 1,200–1,500 litres/day of nutrient-rich digestate → liquid fertiliser for farm
- **CAPEX:** ₹8–12L for 25 m³ plant including gas storage bag, piping to kitchen
- **MNRE subsidy:** ₹7,500–10,000 per unit (household) — for institutional, check MNRE FIRM portal

### Biochar production
- **Feedstock:** farm prunings + coconut shells + palm fronds + sugarcane bagasse from nearby farms
- **Technology options:**
  - Simple TLUD (Top-Lit Updraft) gasifier: ₹80K–1.5L; 200–400 kg/day; suitable for COE training
  - Retort kiln (Adam retort): ₹3–5L; 500–800 kg/day; sellable quality
  - Pyrolysis unit (continuous): ₹15–25L; 1–2 tonnes/day; commercial grade
- **Yield:** ~25–30% weight conversion (1 tonne biomass → 250–300 kg biochar)
- **Revenue:** ₹8–20/kg depending on grade; premium agricultural biochar ₹15–25/kg
- **Applications:** campus soil amendment, sell to Madurai/Virudhunagar farms, nurseries, export via Thoothukudi
- **Carbon credits:** IBI-certified biochar qualifies for Verra VCS carbon credits (~0.8–1.2 tonnes CO₂e/tonne biochar); ₹500–1,500/credit

### Organic farm design (15–20 acres production area)
- **Zones:** 1 acre herbs/greens (intensive, close to kitchen), 4 acres vegetables + legumes, 5 acres orchard (mango/jackfruit/moringa), 3 acres paddy (seeraga samba heritage variety), 2 acres fodder (for cows), 1 acre seed nursery
- **Irrigation:** drip + mulched beds; rainwater harvest from capsule rooftops into sump → farm pump
- **Soil building:** biochar + digestate from biogas + compost from kitchen waste; target 3–4% soil organic carbon in 5 years (from typical 0.4–0.8% in Tamil Nadu agricultural soils)
- **Certifications:** PGS (Participatory Guarantee System) organic in Year 2; NPOP (National Programme for Organic Production) in Year 4; target EU organic export certification by Year 6 for biochar
- **Livestock integration:** 4–6 Kangeyam or Umblachery cows (native breeds); dung → biogas; urine → liquid fertiliser; milk → kitchen (members' breakfast)

### Water management (integrates with Dr. Meera)
- **Greywater treatment:** constructed wetland (reed beds) → polished for drip irrigation
- **Blackwater:** STP → treated effluent → deep-root forest irrigation only
- **Biogas slurry:** diluted 1:10 → drip fertigation for orchard and paddy
- **Compost leachate:** collected sump → farm application

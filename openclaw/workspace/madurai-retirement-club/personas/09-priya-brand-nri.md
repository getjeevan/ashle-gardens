## Knowledge domains

### Brand positioning
- **Primary audience:** NRI Tamil families (US, Singapore, UAE, UK, Canada, Australia, Malaysia)
  - They want: safety, dignity, familiar culture, medical access, peer community, status
  - They fear: parent loneliness, poor healthcare, cultural alienation, staff exploitation of elderly
- **Secondary:** Madurai/TN upper-middle-class retirees seeking active ageing
- **Positioning:** "Country-club retirement living rooted in Madurai's heritage — where your parents live, not just stay."
- **NOT:** an old-age home. Never use those words. It's a Heritage Lifestyle Community.

### Digital strategy
- Instagram: heritage architecture reels, farm-to-table process, sunrise lake content, student stories from COE
- YouTube: "A day at Ashle Gardens" documentary series; heritage Madurai cuisine deep-dives
- WhatsApp broadcast: monthly campus update to NRI families (builds trust over 18-month sales cycle)
- Website: bilingual (Tamil + English); virtual 360° tour; NRI investment calculator
- SEO targets: "best retirement community Madurai", "NRI parents India retirement home", "heritage retirement Tamil Nadu"

### NRI sales process
1. **Awareness** (WhatsApp/Instagram/Tamil Sangam events): share stories, not sales
2. **Nurture** (12–18 months): monthly WhatsApp updates, virtual events, video calls with Founder
3. **Site visit hook**: "Come see your parents' next home on your next India trip" — arrange campus visit
4. **Closing**: 1:1 with Karthik CFO (investment structuring) + Rajan-Anna (title/legal clarity)
5. **Referral**: every buyer gets 3 referral credits (₹25K each on next sale)

### Pricing strategy
- Heritage Suite: ₹95–130L outright; ₹48–65L + ₹38–45K/month lifetime lease
- Capsule Airbnb: ₹4,500–8,000/night; bulk corporate booking ₹3,500/night
- Membership (non-resident): ₹2.5L joining + ₹18K/month — access to lake, kitchen, wellness
- Launch pricing: first 15 units at 15% discount + RFID linen service free for Year 1

## Knowledge domains

### COE structure and legal entity
- **Section 8 Company** (Not-for-profit, Ministry of Corporate Affairs) — best for CSR funding + 80G + FCRA
- **NSDC-affiliated skill centre** — qualifies for PMKVY (Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana) funding
- **NCVT MIS affiliation** — for certificate-level courses (1-year ITI-equivalent)
- **Apprenticeship under Apprentices Act 1961** — can take 10% of workforce as apprentices (legal framework for pay)

### COE program design
**Ashle Gardens Live Campus Apprenticeship (ALCA):**
- Duration: 6–18 months
- Stipend: ₹6,000–8,000/month (covers food + hostel on campus)
- Work component (70%): rotations across laundry, kitchen, farm, biochar, eco-tourism, maintenance
- Learning component (30%): theory, documentation, safety, entrepreneurship
- Certification: joint certificate (Ashle Gardens COE + partner ITI/Polytechnic)
- Conversion: top 30% offered full employment; others placed with B2B partners (hospitals, hotels)

## ⚠️ Philosophy correction (2026-05-16)

The COE is NOT a paid apprenticeship or govt scheme training centre.

**It is a pro bono innovation residency.** Bright young minds — engineering students, researchers, entrepreneurs — come to Ashle Gardens to solve real problems. They get free accommodation (capsule eco-house), free meals (Ashle kitchen), lab access, mentorship, ₹50K prototype budget, and the right to commercialise their solution outside the campus.

Ashle Gardens gets working solutions built by motivated people. Society gets open, transferable knowledge.

**This changes your framing:** Stop positioning the COE as a government-scheme skills-training centre. Position it as the **"Ashle Gardens Innovation Lab"** — where the most interesting young minds in Tamil Nadu come to build something real, in the middle of a living circular economy campus.

Your funding strategy also changes:
- Primary: **CSR sponsorships** from corporates who want specific problems solved (₹5-25L per project)
- Secondary: **DST/ICAR/ICMR research grants** for agricultural/health/biochar projects
- Tertiary: **IP royalties** (3-5%) when a resident commercialises their solution
- NOT primary: PMKVY/NSDC government training fees (though these can supplement for any basic-skills track)

Revise all COE documents to reflect this pro bono innovation model.

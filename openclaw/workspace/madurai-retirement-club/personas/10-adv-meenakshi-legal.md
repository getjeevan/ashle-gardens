## Knowledge domains

### Multi-entity structure for Ashle Gardens
- **Operating company** (main campus, retirement, laundry, kitchen, tourism): Pvt Ltd
  - NRI investment via FDI automatic route (100% in hospitality)
  - RERA registration mandatory before selling any residential units
- **Section 8 Company** (COE): Not-for-profit, eligible for CSR, PMKVY, FCRA
  - MCA21 registration, Objects clause to include skill development + research
  - Separate bank account, audit, 80G application for donor tax benefit
- **Organic farm** (optional FPO — Farmer Producer Organisation):
  - If involving landowners/workers as members; easier NABARD funding
  - Can own land, easier for collective agri-credit
- **NRI SPA / Power of Attorney**: for buyers unable to attend registration in person

### Land conversion (Virudhunagar — J-farm, Kariapatti)
- **Current status:** likely agricultural patta (need to verify chitta, FMB, A-register)
- **Conversion path:** Section 27/28 of TN Land Reforms Act — apply to Kariapatti RDO
- **Required:** Site plan, patta, link documents (last 30 years), soil report, no-encroachment certificate
- **DTCP approval:** layout plan for subdivision; Regional Office, Virudhunagar
- **Timeline:** 6–14 months for conversion; parallel DTCP approval
- **Fee:** ₹30–60/m² (varies by land type and use)
- **Common failure point:** 3rd party patta claims; ensure 30-year link document chain before purchase

### TN RERA (for retirement units)
- **Applicability:** any project >500 sqm OR >8 units must register before advertising or selling
- **Registration:** TNRERA portal (rera.tn.gov.in); 30–60 day process; ₹5/sqft fee
- **Required disclosures:** project plan, DTCP approval, CA certificate of 70% fund in escrow
- **Penalty for violation:** up to 10% of project cost
- **NRI buyer protection:** RERA escrow protects buyers even if developer defaults

### FEMA for NRI buyers and investors
- **NRI purchase of immovable property:** permitted automatically EXCEPT agricultural/plantation/farmhouse land
  - Ashle Gardens retirement units: FEMA automatic route ✓
  - Farm portion: NRI CANNOT directly own agricultural land — must be in Indian company or through resident Indian spouse
- **Repatriation:** NRI can repatriate ₹1 million/year from NRO account; sale proceeds of 2 properties in lifetime
- **OCD structure:** Optionally Convertible Debentures — classified as debt initially (simpler RBI compliance), converts to equity later
- **Filing:** FC-GPR filing with RBI within 30 days of any NRI equity infusion

### Labour law for COE apprentices
- **Apprentices Act 1961:** mandates stipend (NAPS rate: ₹6,000–9,000/month for apprentices); company contribution ₹1,500/month
- **EPFO/ESIC:** apprentices are NOT employees — no PF/ESI mandatory, but recommended as goodwill
- **Conversion to employment:** after 18 months, if offering employment, full statutory compliance begins
- **CLRA (Contract Labour Regulation Act):** if outsourcing any labour (laundry workers, kitchen helpers) — registration required

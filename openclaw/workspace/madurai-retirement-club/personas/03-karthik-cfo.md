## Knowledge domains

### Campus-level financial architecture
- Total CAPEX estimate: ₹45–65 Cr for full build-out over 5 years
- Phase 1 (Y1–2): ₹15–18 Cr — site prep, retirement wing (30 units), laundry, kitchen, biogas
- Phase 2 (Y3–4): ₹18–22 Cr — COE, eco-tourism, biochar, capsule cluster
- Phase 3 (Y5+): ₹12–20 Cr — NRI suites, spa, Phase 2 retirement wing, farm expansion
- Funding mix: 40% NRI equity, 30% promoter, 20% term loan (TN SIDBI/SBI), 10% govt subsidy

### Retirement community unit economics
- Heritage suite (1200 sqft): ₹90–120L outright OR ₹45–65L + ₹35–45K/month LTA lease
- Capsule Airbnb: ₹4,500–8,000/night; target 65% occupancy = ₹1.2–1.8L/capsule/month
- 30 heritage units + 24 capsules: ~₹8–12 Cr/yr revenue at scale

### Commercial laundry unit economics
- 200 kg/hr industrial washer-dryer line: ₹1.2–1.8 Cr CAPEX
- Pricing: ₹15–25/kg; target 2,000 kg/day at maturity = ₹30–50K/day
- Madurai hospital target (50+ hospitals): SLA contracts ₹1.5–2.5L/month each
- Break-even: 600–800 kg/day. Year 1 target: 800 kg. Year 3: 2,000 kg.
- EBITDA margin at scale: 28–35%

### Commercial kitchen unit economics
- Central kitchen CAPEX: ₹80L–1.2 Cr
- Hospital canteen contract: ₹2.5–4L/month (3 meals × 200–400 people)
- Catering (weddings, corporate): ₹200–350/plate, 5–10 events/month
- Cloud kitchen delivery (Swiggy/Zomato): ₹8,000–15,000/day revenue
- Break-even: 3 hospital contracts + 2 catering events/month
- EBITDA margin at scale: 22–30%

### Circular economy value unlocks
- Biogas saves: ₹3–5L/yr in LPG for kitchen
- Biochar revenue: ₹8–15/kg, 200 kg/day = ₹6–11L/month
- Farm (5 acres): saves ₹12–18L/yr in kitchen procurement; surplus ₹4–6L/yr
- COE workforce subsidy: students paid ₹6–8K/month (vs market ₹18–22K) → saves ₹35–50L/yr in labour

### NRI investor structuring (FEMA-compliant)
- Structure: Pvt Ltd with NRI OCB/FDI up to 100% via automatic route (hospitality)
- NRI can repatriate principal + dividend via NRO→NRE transfer (up to $1M/yr)
- Preferred: NRI invests via Optionally Convertible Debentures (OCD) — 12% fixed return for 5 yrs, then equity conversion
- Section 8 for COE: CSR eligible, 80G benefits for donors
- Land in Indian company name (not personal NRI) — avoids inheritance complications

### Funding sources
- TN MSME loan (DIC Virudhunagar): up to ₹2 Cr at 6.5% for MSME-classified laundry/kitchen
- TIDCO / Guidance TN: mega investment single-window if >₹50 Cr total investment
- SBI AgriTech / NABARD: farm + biogas components
- Tourism Ministry (PRASHAD / Swadesh Darshan): eco-tourism infrastructure grant
- NSDC / MSDE: COE establishment grant for skill development

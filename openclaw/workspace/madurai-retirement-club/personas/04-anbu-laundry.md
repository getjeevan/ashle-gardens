### Equipment selection (2,000 kg/day capacity)
- 2 × 100 kg continuous batch washer (Jensen or Milnor) — ₹35–45L each
- 2 × 50 kg pass-through washer for infection-control linen — ₹18–25L each
- 4 × 50 kg gas/electric dryer — ₹8–12L each
- 1 × flatwork ironer (chest ironer, 3m width) — ₹25–35L
- 1 × tunnel finisher for uniforms — ₹15–20L
- RFID linen tracking system (Datamars or Datawedge) — ₹8–12L for 10,000 tags
- Water softener + RO plant (recycling 70% water) — ₹12–18L
- Solar water heater (supplement electric boiler) — ₹8–12L
**Total CAPEX estimate: ₹1.8–2.4 Cr**

### Hospital linen compliance
- NABH standard: clean/dirty separation, no cross-contamination
- Infection control: colour-coded bags (yellow = infected, red = high-risk), autoclaving protocol
- Documentation: wash temperature log (71°C/3min or 65°C/10min minimum), chemical batch records
- Linen life tracking: RFID records wash count per item (hospital linen replacement at 150–200 washes)
- ISO 14001: water recycling >65%, chemical COD limits for effluent, eco-certification

### Differentiation strategy
1. **RFID traceability** — hospital CFOs love per-item wash count reports (reduces theft, replacement cost)
2. **TAT guarantee** — 24-hour turnaround vs 48–72 hr from Madurai city competitors
3. **Eco-certification** — ISO 14001 + solar-heated water → premium for NABH hospitals
4. **On-site pickup/delivery** — 2 dedicated vehicles, GPS tracked, 2 rounds/day
5. **Price lock SLA** — 3-year contract with ₹/kg price fixed (hospitals hate cost volatility)

### Operations
- Staff: 1 supervisor + 12 operators per shift; 2 shifts = 26 staff
- Water: 15–20 litres/kg washed; 2,000 kg/day = 30,000–40,000 litres/day (RO recycle 65%)
- Energy: 0.8–1.2 kWh/kg; biogas supplement for water heating saves 25–30% electricity
- Chemical cost: ₹1.5–2.5/kg washed
- Effluent: STP + ETP required; TNPCB consent; outlet to constructed wetland (integrate with Dr. Meera's wetland design)

## Knowledge domains

### ☀️ Madurai climate & geography
- **Latitude/longitude:** ~9.92°N 78.12°E, elevation ~140m
- **Climate:** BSh (semi-arid hot); annual mean 28°C; April–June peaks 38–42°C; Dec–Jan lows 18–22°C
- **Rainfall:** ~850 mm/yr; NE monsoon (Oct–Dec) dominant (~55%), SW monsoon (Jun–Sep) ~30%
- **Soils:** Red sandy loam in higher terrain (Tirupparankundram, Sivagangai border), black cotton (vertisol) in lower plains, alluvial near Vaigai
- **Hydrology:** Vaigai river basin; tanks/ery system (ancient Chola/Pandiyan tank irrigation), declining water table (now ~150–250 ft for borewells in outskirts)
- **Vegetation zone:** Tropical thorn forest transitioning to dry deciduous

### 💧 Water design (lake + island + streams)
- **3-acre lake target capacity:** if avg depth 4m → ~48,000 m³. Catchment needed (rule of thumb): ~10× lake area for monsoon-fed = 30 acres of catchment, OR sized borewell recharge wells
- **Lake construction:** bund design (1:2 slope), clay puddling vs HDPE liner cost/benefit, spillway weir, silt traps at inlets
- **1-acre island access:** two pedestrian causeways (200m apart) at finished water level; a small jetty for a non-motorized boat; cable ferry option for character
- **Water stream around capsule houses:** recirculating loop fed by lake; pumping budget ~2 kWh/day per 10m³ throughput; bio-swale filtration before return
- **Rainwater harvesting:** every roof to harvest, target capacity 1.25× monthly demand for May–Sep, rooftop-to-tank with leaf filter + first-flush diverter
- **Greywater:** reed-bed wetland (Typha, Phragmites), tertiary polish pond, reused for landscape only
- **Blackwater:** STP sized for full occupancy + 20%; design for HRAS (high-rate activated sludge) for footprint; treated effluent to deep root irrigation only (not lake)
- **Lake registration:** PWD permission required; lake >1 acre in Tamil Nadu typically needs district environmental clearance

### 🏛️ Heritage Madurai room inspiration
- **Mutram (central courtyard)** — open-to-sky, holds tulasi maadam, drains via pavugal channels
- **Thinnai (raised verandah)** — semi-public seating, social interface, cooling buffer
- **Padikattu (stepped plinth)** — flood resilience, ritual entry
- **Murugu / wood column profiles** — teak (slow-growth) or vembu (neem, native)
- **Naalukettu / ettukettu plans** — 4 or 8 inward-facing wings around courtyard; perfect retirement-suite layout
- **Athangudi tile / Chettinad lime plaster** — heritage flooring, cool surfaces
- **Madurai-specific motifs:** Meenakshi temple gopuram filigree, kolam patterns, kalamkari murals
- **Vasthu alignment:** entry east/north, kitchen southeast (agni mool), bedroom southwest (master)

### 🏠 Capsule houses for Airbnb
- **Modalities to evaluate:**
  - Ferro-cement vaulted shells (Auroville model) — 4m × 6m, ₹3–4 lakh/unit, lifespan 30+ yrs
  - Earthbag (super-adobe) — cheaper, harder permit, beautiful aesthetic
  - Shipping container + thatch overlay — fast deploy but hot without thick insulation
  - Bamboo + bamboo-crete — INBAR-spec, ₹2–3 lakh/unit, 15-year frame life
- **Passive cooling layer:**
  - Thick walls (250–350mm) for thermal mass, OR
  - Double-skin roof with 100mm ventilated air gap
  - Cross-ventilation on the prevailing SW–NE summer axis
  - Earth-tube cooling: 30m underground pipe, drops air 4–6°C, near-zero opex
- **Cluster pattern:** 8 capsules around a small fountain courtyard, 25m diameter; total 4 clusters around the lake

### 🌱 Native flora palette (zone planting)
- **Zone 1 (around kitchen, ≤30m):** curry leaf (Murraya koenigii / kariveppilai), drumstick (Moringa oleifera / murunga), banana (Musa / vazhai), tulasi, lemon, betel-leaf vine
- **Zone 2 (food forest, 30–100m):** mango (Mangifera indica / mavu), jackfruit (Artocarpus / palaa), tamarind (Tamarindus / puli), guava (Psidium / koyya), pomegranate (Punica / madhulai), wood-apple (Limonia / vilampazham)
- **Zone 3 (shade + biomass canopy, 100–300m):** neem (Azadirachta / vembu), pongam (Pongamia / pungai), Indian mast tree (Polyalthia longifolia), Indian rosewood (Dalbergia sissoo / sissoo), iluppai (Madhuca longifolia)
- **Zone 4 (wild edge):** marudham (Terminalia arjuna), athi (Ficus racemosa), Indian elm (Holoptelea / aaya-maram), thespesia (Thespesia populnea / poovarasu)
- **Avoid:** Eucalyptus, Casuarina monoculture, Acacia auriculiformis (invasive)

### 🦋 Biodiversity targets (5-year)

- Pollinator garden (5+ native flowering species in continuous bloom across seasons)
- Otter / fish kingfisher reintroduction in lake (post Year 3)
- No-pesticide policy; IPM only

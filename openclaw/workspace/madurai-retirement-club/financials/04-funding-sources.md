# Funding Sources — Ashle Gardens
*Karthik CFO | Phase-wise funding roadmap*

---

## Phase 0 (₹65–85L) — Founder + Seed NRI

### 1. Founder Equity
- Amount: ₹40–50L
- Structure: ordinary shares in Ashle Gardens Pvt Ltd
- Return: equity appreciation + salary as MD

### 2. Single NRI Seed Investor (OCD structure)
- Amount: ₹20–35L
- Instrument: Optionally Convertible Debentures (OCD)
- Terms: 12% per annum fixed for 3 years, then option to convert to equity at pre-agreed valuation (typically 20× EBITDA at conversion)
- FEMA compliance: OCD is treated as debt (ECB route) — FC-GPR filing with RBI within 30 days
- Why OCD: simpler RBI compliance than equity; gives investor downside protection; founder retains control

**Target investor profile:** Tamil diaspora professional (Singapore/US), ₹30–40L investment, knows the founder personally, wants "foot in the door" before larger Phase 2 round.

## Phase 1 (₹1.8–2.5 Cr) — Debt + Operating Cash Flow

### 3. DIC MSME Loan — Virudhunagar District Industries Centre
- Amount: up to **₹2 Cr**
- Rate: **6.5% per annum** (current rate under MSME Credit Guarantee Fund)
- Tenure: 5–7 years
- Collateral: CGTMSE covers loans up to ₹2 Cr — **NO collateral needed**
- Where to apply: DIC Office, Virudhunagar + preferred lender (Canara Bank or Indian Bank at DIC tie-up branch)
- Pre-requisites: Udyam registration (free on udyamregistration.gov.in), 3 months of GST returns, hospital SLA contracts as proof of revenue pipeline
- Processing time: 6–8 weeks

### 4. NABARD Schemes (Farm + Biogas)
- **DEDS (Dairy Entrepreneurship Development Scheme):** 25% back-end subsidy for cow shed + biogas linked to dairy. Per unit: ₹3.3L subsidy. For 6 cows: ₹3.3L + ₹3L biogas = ₹6.3L from government
- **RIDF (Rural Infrastructure Development Fund):** For farm infrastructure (irrigation, storage). Up to ₹50L at 6.5%
- Contact: NABARD District Development Manager, Virudhunagar. Apply through: Bank of India/SBI agri branch

### 5. MNRE Biogas Subsidy
- Scheme: National Biogas and Organic Manure Programme (NBOMP)
- Subsidy: ₹10,000–14,000 per m³ for institutional plants (>25 m³)
- For 25 m³ plant: **₹2.5–3.5L government subsidy** (reduces CAPEX from ₹10L to ₹6.5–7.5L)
- Apply through: State Nodal Agency = Tamil Nadu Energy Development Agency (TEDA), Chennai
- Processing: 2–3 months

## Phase 2 (₹3–4 Cr) — NRI OCD Round + Tourism Grants

### 6. NRI OCD Investment Round
- Target: 3–5 Tamil diaspora investors
- Amount per investor: ₹50–75L
- Total target: ₹1.5–3 Cr
- Instrument: OCD at 12% fixed for 3 years, equity conversion option
- Target geographies: US (New Jersey, California Tamil communities), Singapore (Tamil professionals), UAE (Dubai Tamil business community)
- Pitch tool: NRI Investor Deck (see financials/03-nri-investor-deck-outline.md)
- Channel: Tamil Sangams, direct referral from Phase 0 investor

### 7. Swadesh Darshan 2.0 — Ministry of Tourism
- Scheme: Tourism infrastructure grant for heritage + eco-tourism
- Amount: up to ₹1.5 Cr for qualifying projects
- Eligibility: Ashle Gardens qualifies as Heritage Eco-tourism — proximity to Madurai Meenakshi temple (heritage circuit), eco-campus
- Apply through: TN Tourism Department → Ministry of Tourism
- Required: DTCP approval, TN Tourism classification, DPR (Detailed Project Report)
- Processing: 6–12 months; competitive selection

### 8. PRASHAD Scheme — Ministry of Tourism
- Scheme: Pilgrimage Rejuvenation and Spiritual Heritage Augmentation Drive
- Eligibility: Madurai is a PRASHAD site (Meenakshi temple). Ashle Gardens as pilgrimage tourism support facility qualifies
- Grant: Infrastructure for hospitality near pilgrimage sites
- Apply: Same channel as Swadesh Darshan

## Ongoing — CSR Funding (for COE Innovation Lab)

### 9. Sivakasi Fireworks Industry CSR
- Mandatory CSR: Companies with net profit >₹5 Cr must spend 2% on CSR (Companies Act Schedule VII)
- Key companies: Standard Fireworks, Sri Kaliswari, Coronation Industries, Sony Kiran
- Combined annual CSR budget estimate: ₹15–25 Cr across Sivakasi industry
- Pitch to them: COE trains rural youth in chemical safety + industrial safety = direct CSR alignment
- Expected per company: ₹25–75L for a named lab or program

### 10. Chennai/Bengaluru IT Companies
- Infosys, TCS, Cognizant have Madurai centres with CSR obligations
- Pitch: Co-working facility + digital skills training at COE
- Expected: ₹10–30L per company annually

### 11. Madurai Textile/Garment Industry
- KKDIK, Madura Garments — significant CSR budgets
- Pitch: Laundry technology + textile skills training at COE

## Ongoing — Government Grants for COE

### 12. PMKVY 4.0 (NSDC)
- Per student payout: ₹15,000–35,000 depending on sector
  - Hospitality: ₹15,000 | Agriculture: ₹12,000 | Advanced manufacturing: ₹28,000
- For 100 students/year: **₹15–35L/year from NSDC**
- How to get it: Register as NSDC Training Partner on smartnsdc.org → centre affiliation (minimum 1,000 sqft, qualified assessors) → batch proposal → training → assessment → payout

### 13. TNSDC — CM Kaushal Pattarai
- Tamil Nadu Skill Development Corporation scheme
- ₹10,000–20,000 per student
- Apply through: TNSDC district coordinator, Virudhunagar

### 14. DST — NIDHI Programme
- For innovation and entrepreneurship ecosystem
- Seed support for startups incubated at COE
- Up to ₹50L for qualifying technology startups from the lab

### 15. ICAR Research Grants
- For biochar + soil health + sustainable agriculture research at COE farm
- Apply through ICAR-TNAU Coimbatore regional office
- Amount: ₹5–15L per project
